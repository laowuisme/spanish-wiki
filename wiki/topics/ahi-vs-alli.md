## Rule

Spanish has three words for "there," marking increasing distance from the speaker:

| Word | Distance | Equivalent demonstrative |
|---|---|---|
| *aquí* | here — near speaker | este/esta |
| *ahí* | there — near listener | ese/esa |
| *allí / allá* | over there — far from both | aquel/aquella |

> *¿Está ahí?* — Is it there (near you)?
> *Hay menos gente allí.* — There are fewer people over there.

**Allí vs allá:** Both mean "over there." *Allí* is more precise (a fixed place); *allá* is vaguer or implies movement (*Voy allá* = I'm going over there). *Allí* is more common in Spain; *allá* is more common in Latin America.

## Common Patterns

| Example | Translation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| *Ponlo ahí.* | Put it there. | Near the listener |
| *Vive allí.* | She lives over there. | Far from both |
| *Ven aquí.* | Come here. | Near the speaker |
| *Por allá.* | Over that way. | Approximate direction |
