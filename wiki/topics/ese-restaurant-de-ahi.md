# Demonstrative Patterns — este/ese/aquel

## Rule

Spanish has three levels of demonstratives, reflecting distance from the speaker:

| Distance | Masc sg | Fem sg | Masc pl | Fem pl | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Near speaker | este | esta | estos | estas | this / these |
| Near listener | ese | esa | esos | esas | that / those |
| Far from both | aquel | aquella | aquellos | aquellas | that (over there) |

### este vs aquél (note from raw_log1)
- `este` = this (near me)
- `aquel` = that (far away, over there) — note: **aquel**, not "aqueal"

### aquel vs aquella
- `aquel` (masculine): `aquel hombre` — that man over there
- `aquella` (feminine): `aquella mujer` — that woman over there

### estos/estas vs esos/esas
- `estos/estas` = these (near speaker)
- `esos/esas` = those (near listener or moderately far)

### Reinforcing with location adverbs
`Ese restaurante de ahí.` — That restaurant over there (near you).
Adding `de ahí` (over there/there) or `de aquí` (here) reinforces the location.

## Common Patterns

| Example | Translation |
|---|---|
| `este musical` (masculine) | this musical |
| `esta canción` (feminine) | this song |
| `Ese restaurante de ahí.` | That restaurant (over there). |
| `aquel edificio` | that building (far away) |
| `aquella mujer` | that woman (far away) |

## Your Mistakes

- `este musical` (not `esta`) — musical is masculine.
- `este vs aqueal` — the correct form is `aquel`, not `aqueal`.
- `aquel vs aquella` — need to match gender of noun.
