## Rule

*Sorprendido* and *sorprendente* both come from *sorprender* (to surprise), but they describe different things:

**sorprendido/a** — surprised (how a person feels)
- Past participle used as adjective. Describes the **person** who receives the surprise.
- Used with *estar*: *Estoy sorprendido.* — I am surprised.
- Agrees in gender/number: *sorprendida* (fem.), *sorprendidos* (masc. pl.)

**sorprendente** — surprising (a quality of something)
- Present participle used as adjective. Describes the **thing** that causes surprise.
- Can be used with *ser* or *estar*: *Es sorprendente.* — It's surprising.
- No gender change: *sorprendente* for both masc./fem.

## The Pattern

This same -ido / -ente contrast applies to many emotion adjectives:

| Feeling | Person feels... | Thing is... |
|---|---|---|
| surprise | *sorprendido/a* | *sorprendente* |
| interest | *interesado/a* | *interesante* |
| boredom | *aburrido/a* | *aburrido/a* (same form) |
| fascination | *fascinado/a* | *fascinante* |
