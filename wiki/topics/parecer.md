# Parecer — gustar-type verb ("to seem")

## Rule

`parecer` ("to seem," "to appear") behaves like `gustar`: the thing that seems a certain way is the grammatical subject, and the person to whom it seems is marked with an indirect object pronoun (me/te/le/nos/os/les), optionally clarified with `a + person` (see [[indirect-object-a-pronoun]]).

`[IOP] + parecer + adjective`

Like other gustar-type verbs ([[gusta-followed-by-clause]]), `parecer` agrees in number with a plural noun subject (*Me parecen caros* — They seem expensive to me), but stays singular (`parece`) before an adjective describing a general situation.

## Common Patterns

| Pattern | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| me/te/le parece + adjective | *Me parece raro.* | It seems strange to me. |
| ¿a ti no te parece + adjective? | *¿A ti no te parece extraño?* | Doesn't it seem strange to you? |
| te parece + adjective + noun | *Te parece espantoso este color.* | This color seems dreadful to you. |
