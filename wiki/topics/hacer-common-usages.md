# hacer — Common Usages

## Rule

`hacer` literally means "to do / to make" but it appears in many fixed expressions where English uses completely different verbs. Knowing these fixed uses is essential.

### Core meaning
- `hacer algo` — to do something / to make something
- `¿Qué haces?` — What are you doing?

### Weather expressions (hacer + noun)
- `Hace sol.` — It's sunny.
- `Hace frío.` — It's cold.
- `Hace calor.` — It's hot.
- `Hace viento.` — It's windy.

### Time expressions
- `Hace + time + que + present` — expressing how long something has been going on
  - `Hace dos años que vivo aquí.` — I've been living here for two years.
- `Hace + time + que + preterite` — expressing how long ago something happened
  - `Hace dos años que llegué.` — I arrived two years ago.

### Idiomatic expressions
- `hacer la compra` — to do the grocery shopping
- `hacer la maleta` — to pack a suitcase
- `hacer ejercicio` — to exercise
- `hacer preguntas` — to ask questions
- `hacer caso` — to pay attention / listen
- `hacer falta` — to be needed / to be lacking

## Common Patterns

| Expression | Translation |
|---|---|
| `¿Qué haces?` | What are you doing? |
| `Hace sol / frío / calor` | It's sunny / cold / hot |
| `hacer la compra` | to do the shopping |
| `Hace dos años que...` | It's been two years since... |
