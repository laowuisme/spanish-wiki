# qué Exclamations — ¡Qué lástima!, ¡Qué sorpresa!, ¡Qué lindo!

## Rule

`¡Qué + [noun / adjective]!` is a highly productive Spanish exclamatory pattern. The structure is extremely simple: `qué` + any noun or adjective expresses a strong reaction. No verb is required.

### Pattern: ¡Qué + noun!

| Expression | Translation |
|---|---|
| `¡Qué lástima!` | What a shame! |
| `¡Qué sorpresa!` | What a surprise! |
| `¡Qué desastre!` | What a disaster! |
| `¡Qué pena!` | What a pity! / That's too bad! |
| `¡Qué emoción!` | How exciting! |

### Pattern: ¡Qué + adjective!

| Expression | Translation |
|---|---|
| `¡Qué feo!` | How ugly! |
| `¡Qué lindo!` | How cute/lovely! |
| `¡Qué tonto!` | How silly! / What an idiot! |
| `¡Qué rico!` | How delicious! |

### Adding a noun: ¡Qué + adj + noun!
`¡Qué lindo perro!` — What a lovely dog!

### Related fixed exclamations
- `¡Qué lástima!` — shame
- `Basta de chismes.` — Enough gossip. (Basta = enough/stop)
- `¡Qué haces!` — What are you doing! (can also be exclamatory, not just interrogative)

## Common Patterns

Use `¡Qué + [word]!` freely for any strong emotional reaction. This pattern does not change for gender or number.
