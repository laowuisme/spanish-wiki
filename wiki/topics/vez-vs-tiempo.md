## Rule

Both *vez* and *tiempo* translate as "time" in English, but they are not interchangeable.

**vez** — a countable instance or occurrence of something happening
> *una vez* — once / one time
> *muchas veces* — many times / often
> *a veces* — sometimes
> *otra vez* — again

**tiempo** — time as an uncountable quantity or duration; also weather
> *No tengo tiempo.* — I don't have time.
> *¿Cuánto tiempo llevas aquí?* — How long have you been here?
> *El tiempo pasa rápido.* — Time passes quickly.

**The test:** Can you count it? → *vez*. Is it an abstract quantity or duration? → *tiempo*.

## Common Patterns

| Phrase | Word | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *una vez* (once) | vez | countable instance |
| *muchas veces* (often) | vez | countable repetitions |
| *a veces* (sometimes) | vez | countable occurrences |
| *¿Tienes tiempo?* (Do you have time?) | tiempo | abstract quantity |
| *mucho tiempo* (a long time) | tiempo | uncountable duration |
| *a tiempo* (on time) | tiempo | abstract concept |
| *el tiempo libre* (free time) | tiempo | duration/availability |

**Note:** *tiempo* also means weather (*Hace buen tiempo* = The weather is nice). *Vez* never means weather.
