# Past Tense — Preterite vs Imperfect

## Rule

Spanish splits what English calls "the past tense" into two moods. Picking the right one — not the endings — is the hard part for an English speaker.

### 1. Preterite (Pretérito Indefinido) — completed actions

Regular endings:

| Person | -ar (hablar) | -er (comer) | -ir (vivir) |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | hablé | comí | viví |
| tú | hablaste | comiste | viviste |
| él/ella | habló | comió | vivió |
| nosotros | hablamos | comimos | vivimos |
| vosotros | hablasteis | comisteis | vivisteis |
| ellos | hablaron | comieron | vivieron |

Use the preterite for: a single completed action, an action with a clear start/end, a sequence of completed events, or something that happened a specific number of times.

### 2. Imperfect (Pretérito Imperfecto) — background, habitual, ongoing

Regular endings:

| Person | -ar (hablar) | -er (comer) | -ir (vivir) |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | hablaba | comía | vivía |
| tú | hablabas | comías | vivías |
| él/ella | hablaba | comía | vivía |
| nosotros | hablábamos | comíamos | vivíamos |
| vosotros | hablabais | comíais | vivíais |
| ellos | hablaban | comían | vivían |

Use the imperfect for: habitual/repeated past actions ("used to..."), descriptions (weather, age, time, physical/emotional state), an ongoing background action, or "was/were -ing".

**Only 3 verbs are irregular in the imperfect, in all of Spanish:**

| | ser | ir | ver |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | era | iba | veía |
| tú | eras | ibas | veías |
| él/ella | era | iba | veía |
| nosotros | éramos | íbamos | veíamos |
| vosotros | erais | ibais | veíais |
| ellos | eran | iban | veían |

Everything else you'll ever conjugate in the imperfect is fully regular.

### Contrast

- `Cuando era niño, jugaba en el parque.` — When I was a kid, I used to play in the park. (imperfect: habitual/background)
- `Ayer jugué en el parque.` — Yesterday I played in the park. (preterite: one completed event)
- `Estaba cocinando cuando sonó el teléfono.` — I was cooking when the phone rang. (imperfect sets the scene; preterite interrupts it — this pairing is extremely common)

## Common Patterns

Trigger words that usually signal one mood over the other:

| Preterite triggers | Imperfect triggers |
|---|---|
| ayer, anoche | siempre, todos los días |
| la semana pasada | mientras |
| de repente | cuando era niño/a |
| una vez, dos veces | a menudo, normalmente |
