# se ve — It Looks / It Seems

## Rule

`Se ve + adjective` is a common impersonal construction meaning "it looks (adjective)" or "it seems (adjective)." It uses the reflexive `se` with the third person singular of `ver` (to see).

- `Se ve delicioso.` — It looks delicious.
- `Se ve difícil.` — It looks difficult.
- `Se ve interesante.` — It looks interesting.

This is part of a broader pattern of impersonal `se` constructions:

### Broader se constructions

| Construction | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `se ve + adj` | looks/seems | `Se ve rico.` — It looks tasty. |
| `se dice` | it is said / people say | `Se dice que...` — They say that... |
| `se puede` | one can / you can | `Se puede entrar.` — You can go in. |
| `se vende` | for sale | `Se vende.` — For sale. |

`verdad` — can be used as a tag question: `Se ve bien, ¿verdad?` — It looks good, doesn't it?

## Common Patterns

- `Se ve + adjective` = it looks/seems
- `¿verdad?` appended = "isn't it? / right?"
