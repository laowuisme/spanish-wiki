## Rule

Both *lengua* and *idioma* mean "language," but they have different nuances and register:

| Word | Gender | Primary meaning | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| *la lengua* | feminine | language; tongue (body part) | native language, mother tongue, formal/academic contexts |
| *el idioma* | masculine | language (as a system of communication) | everyday speech, foreign languages, courses |

In practice, they are largely interchangeable when talking about learning languages, but:
- *Lengua materna* = mother tongue (not *idioma materno*)
- *¿Cuántos idiomas hablas?* — How many languages do you speak? (more natural in conversation)
- *La lengua española* — The Spanish language (more formal/literary)

## Common Patterns

| Example | Translation |
|---|---|
| *Estudio el idioma español.* | I'm studying the Spanish language. |
| *Mi lengua materna es el chino.* | My mother tongue is Chinese. |
| *Habla tres idiomas.* | She speaks three languages. |
| *Las lenguas románicas* | The Romance languages |
