## Rule

Both are formal imperatives of *disculpar* (to excuse/forgive), used when addressing strangers politely. The difference is the number of people you're addressing:

| Form | Addresses | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| *disculpe* | one person (usted) | speaking to a single stranger/authority |
| *disculpen* | multiple people (ustedes) | speaking to a group |

> *Disculpe, ¿dónde está el baño?* — Excuse me (to one person), where is the bathroom?
> *Disculpen la demora.* — Sorry for the delay (to a group).

**vs *perdón*:** *Perdón* is the most casual and universally understood. *Disculpe/disculpen* is more formal. See [[perdón-vs-lo-siento]] for the full comparison.

## Common Patterns

| Example | Translation |
|---|---|
| *Disculpe, ¿puede ayudarme?* | Excuse me, can you help me? |
| *Disculpen las molestias.* | We apologise for the inconvenience. (group) |
| *Disculpa* (informal tú) | Sorry / Excuse me (to a friend) |
