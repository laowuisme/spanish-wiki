## Rule

*Antes de* + infinitive means "before doing something." The verb that follows a preposition in Spanish is always in the infinitive form — never conjugated or an -ing form like English "before arriving."

> *Antes de llegar.* — Before arriving.

## Common Patterns

| Pattern | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| antes de + infinitive | *Antes de comer, lávate las manos.* | Before eating, wash your hands. |
| después de + infinitive | *Después de llegar, llamó a su madre.* | After arriving, she called her mother. |
