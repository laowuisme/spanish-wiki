## Rule

| Word | Meaning | Refers to |
|---|---|---|
| *nada* | nothing, not anything | things, concepts, actions |
| *nadie* | nobody, no one, not anyone | people |

Think of them as negatives of *algo* (something) and *alguien* (someone). See [[algo-series]].

> *No hay nada en la nevera.* — There's nothing in the fridge. (thing)
> *No hay nadie en casa.* — There's nobody at home. (person)

Both follow the double negation rule — see [[nadie-nada-negation]].

## Common Patterns

| Pattern | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| no + verb + nada | *No entiendo nada.* | I don't understand anything. |
| no + verb + nadie | *No conozco a nadie.* | I don't know anyone. |
| nada + verb | *Nada funciona.* | Nothing works. |
| nadie + verb | *Nadie sabe.* | Nobody knows. |

**Personal a with nadie:** Because *nadie* refers to people, it takes the personal *a* when used as a direct object: *No veo a nadie* (I don't see anyone). See [[personal-a]].
