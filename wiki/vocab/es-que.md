**Meaning:** the thing is, it's just that (introduces an explanation or excuse)

**Pattern:** Discourse marker used to justify or explain something just said, often softening bad news or a refusal.

**Example:** *No pude venir — es que tuve una emergencia en el trabajo.* — I couldn't come — the thing is, I had an emergency at work.
