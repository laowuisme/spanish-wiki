**Meaning:** when

**Pattern:** Conjunction/relative adverb introducing a time clause (no accent). Contrast with the interrogative *cuándo* (accented, question word) and with *cuánto* (how much/many) — see [[cuanto-vs-cuando]].

**Example:** *Te llamo cuando llegue.* — I'll call you when I arrive.
