**Meaning:** Mr., gentleman’s, sir

**Pattern:** Masculine noun/title: `el señor`, abbreviated `Sr.`; used before a man's name or as a polite form of address.

**Example:** *El señor Pérez llegó tarde.* — Mr. Pérez arrived late.
