**Meaning:** marriage; also "married couple"

**Pattern:** Refers to the institution/state of being married or to the couple itself — distinct from `boda` (the wedding event, not yet in the wiki).

**Example:** *Llevan diez años de matrimonio.* — They've been married for ten years.
