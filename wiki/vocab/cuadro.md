**Meaning:** painting, picture (framed artwork); can also mean "square" or "chart" in other contexts

**Pattern:** In a home/decor context, `cuadro` most often refers to a framed painting or picture hung on a wall.

**Example:** *Colgó un cuadro nuevo en la sala.* — She hung a new painting in the living room.
