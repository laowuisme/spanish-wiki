**Meaning:** congratulations; always plural in Spanish (like "felicidades")

**Pattern:** Used as a standalone exclamation or with `por`: `¡Felicitaciones por + noun/infinitive!`

**Example:** *¡Felicitaciones por ganar el torneo!* — Congratulations on winning the tournament!
