**Meaning:** cooked (past participle of cocer used as adjective); opposite of [[crudo]]

**Pattern:** From the irregular verb cocer (o→ue: cuezo, cueces...); cocido is its past participle.

**Example:** *Prefiero el huevo cocido, no crudo.* — I prefer the egg cooked, not raw.
