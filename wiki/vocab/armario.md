**Meaning:** wardrobe, closet, cabinet, cupboard

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el armario`. Context-dependent: `armario de ropa` (wardrobe), `armario de cocina` (kitchen cabinet). Related: `el clóset` (closet, more common in Latin America).

**Example:** *La ropa está en el armario del dormitorio.* — The clothes are in the bedroom wardrobe.
