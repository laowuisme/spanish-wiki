**Meaning:** to sunbathe, to get some sun

**Pattern:** Here [[tomar]] means "to take", not "to drink": `tomar el sol`. Compare `hace sol` (it is sunny) with [[sol]].

**Example:** *Los domingos tomamos el sol en la playa.* — On Sundays we sunbathe at the beach.
