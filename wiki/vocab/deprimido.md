**Meaning:** depressed, down

**Pattern:** Past participle of `deprimir` used as adjective with `estar` (resultant state) — see [[ser-vs-estar]]. Raw note had this misspelled as "depremido"; corrected here.

**Example:** *Se sintió deprimido después de perder el partido.* — He felt depressed after losing the game.
