**Meaning:** note it down, write it down — imperative of *anotar*

**Pattern:** *Anotar* is a regular -ar verb. *Anota* = tú imperative (informal command). *Anote* = usted imperative (formal). Related to *apuntar* (to jot down).

**Example:** *Anota tu número de teléfono.* — Write down your phone number.
