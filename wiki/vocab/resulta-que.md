**Meaning:** it turns out that...

**Pattern:** Impersonal construction with `resultar` (to turn out/result), similar in form to `parece que` (it seems that). No subject pronoun — the `que`-clause is the subject.

**Example:** *Resulta que no era verdad.* — It turns out it wasn't true.
