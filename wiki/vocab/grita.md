**Meaning:** shouts, yells (3rd person singular present of *gritar*, to shout)

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb: *grito, gritas, grita, gritamos, gritáis, gritan*.

**Example:** *El niño grita cuando tiene miedo.* — The boy shouts when he's scared.
