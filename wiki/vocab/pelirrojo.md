**Meaning:** redhead

**Pattern:** Adjective meaning "red-haired" — agrees in gender/number: `pelirrojo/pelirroja/pelirrojos/pelirrojas`. Often used as a noun for "a redhead."

**Example:** *Mi hermana es pelirroja.* — My sister is a redhead.
