**Meaning:** rent, rental (m); rental price

**Pattern:** *pagar el alquiler* = to pay the rent; *alquilar* = to rent (verb); *el alquiler está muy caro* = rent is very expensive.

**Example:** *El alquiler en esta ciudad es muy caro.* — Rent in this city is very expensive.
