**Meaning:** twins (los gemelos, masculine plural; feminine: gemelas)

**Pattern:** Regular noun. Note: `gemelos` can also mean "cufflinks" or "binoculars" depending on context.

**Example:** *Mis primos son gemelos.* — My cousins are twins.
