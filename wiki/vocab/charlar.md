**Meaning:** to chat, to have a conversation, to talk casually

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. *charla, charlas, charla.* More informal than *hablar*. *Una charla* (noun) = a chat, a talk/lecture. *Tener una charla* = to have a chat.

**Example:** *Estuvimos charlando toda la tarde.* — We were chatting all afternoon.
