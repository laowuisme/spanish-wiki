**Meaning:** package, parcel; packet

**Pattern:** Masculine noun (*el paquete*). Used for parcels sent by post and also for packets of food (e.g., *un paquete de galletas* = a packet of biscuits). *Enviar un paquete* = to send a package.

**Example:** *Tengo que recoger un paquete en la oficina de correos.* — I need to pick up a package at the post office.
