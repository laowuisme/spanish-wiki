**Meaning:** cookie, biscuit, cracker

**Pattern:** Feminine noun. `una galleta`. In Latin America often means any small baked snack; in Spain more specifically a biscuit.

**Example:** *Tomé un café con galletas.* — I had coffee with cookies.
