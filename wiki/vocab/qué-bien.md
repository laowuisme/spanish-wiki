**Meaning:** great

**Pattern:** [[qué-exclamations]] pattern: *¡Qué + adjective/adverb!* → *¡Qué bien!* — informal exclamation of approval at good news.

**Example:** *¡Qué bien que ganaste el partido!* — How great that you won the game!
