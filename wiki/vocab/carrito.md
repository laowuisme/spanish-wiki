**Meaning:** shopping cart, trolley; small cart

**Pattern:** Diminutive of *carro* ([[carro]]). *El carrito de la compra* = shopping trolley. *Carrito* can also mean baby stroller in some Latin American countries.

**Example:** *¿Necesitas un carrito?* — Do you need a trolley?
