**Meaning:** shares (3rd person singular present of *compartir*, to share)

**Pattern:** Regular -ir verb: *comparto, compartes, comparte, compartimos, compartís, comparten*.

**Example:** *Ella comparte su comida con su hermano.* — She shares her food with her brother.
