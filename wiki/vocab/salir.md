**Meaning:** to leave, to go out, to exit

**Pattern:** Common irregular verb. First person singular present: `salgo` (not `salo`). Used for leaving places and going out socially.

**Example:** *Salgo a las ocho.* — I leave at eight. / *¿Quieres salir?* — Do you want to go out?
