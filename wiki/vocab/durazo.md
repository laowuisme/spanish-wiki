**Meaning:** peach (fruit)

**Pattern:** Masculine noun. Regional variants: `durazno` (most of Latin America), `melocotón` (Spain). `durazo` is common in Mexico, Colombia, and parts of Central America.

**Example:** *Me encanta el jugo de durazo.* — I love peach juice.
