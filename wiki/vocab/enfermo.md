**Meaning:** sick

**Pattern:** Agrees in gender: *enfermo/enferma*. Used with *estar* for the temporary state: *estar enfermo* = to be sick. As a noun, means "patient".

**Example:** *Estoy enfermo hoy y no puedo ir a trabajar.* — I'm sick today and can't go to work.
