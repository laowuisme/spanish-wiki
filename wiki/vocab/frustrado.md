**Meaning:** frustrated (adj); *frustrado* (m) / *frustrada* (f)

**Pattern:** *estar frustrado/a* = to feel frustrated (state). Past participle of *frustrar* used as adjective.

**Example:** *Me siento frustrada porque no entiendo la gramática.* — I feel frustrated because I don't understand the grammar.
