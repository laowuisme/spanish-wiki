**Meaning:** spill, spillage; also: levy, surcharge (financial context)

**Pattern:** From verb `derramar` (to spill). As a noun: `la derrama` = the spill / a special levy.

**Example:** *Hay una derrama en el suelo.* — There's a spill on the floor.
