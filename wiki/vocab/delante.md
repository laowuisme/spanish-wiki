**Meaning:** in front, ahead

**Pattern:** Adverb of place. Usually combined with `de` to specify what it's in front of: `delante de + [noun]`.

**Example:** *El coche de mi vecino está delante de mi casa.* — My neighbor's car is in front of my house.
