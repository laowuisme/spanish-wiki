**Meaning:** exhausted, drained, worn out (adj); *agotado* (m) / *agotada* (f); also: sold out (stock)

**Pattern:** *estar agotado/a* = to be exhausted (state). Also used for stock: *el producto está agotado* = the product is sold out. From *agotar* = to exhaust/deplete.

**Example:** *Estoy totalmente agotada después de trabajar doce horas.* — I'm completely exhausted after working twelve hours.
