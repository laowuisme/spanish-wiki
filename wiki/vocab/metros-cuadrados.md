**Meaning:** square metres (m²)

**Pattern:** Plural of `metro cuadrado` (`cuadrado` = square). Used for the size of a flat or house: `un piso de 80 metros cuadrados`. [[metro]] also means subway, so the word after it matters. See [[tamaño]].

**Example:** *El piso tiene setenta metros cuadrados.* — The flat has seventy square metres.
