**Meaning:** to describe

**Pattern:** Irregular verb conjugated like `escribir` (past participle `descrito`, not `describido`). In the affirmative tú command, double object pronouns attach together: `descríbemela` = "describe her/it to me" (me = indirect object, la = feminine direct object). See [[affirmative-commands]].

**Example:** *No conozco a tu hermana — descríbemela.* — I don't know your sister — describe her to me.
