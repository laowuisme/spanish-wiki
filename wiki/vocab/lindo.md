**Meaning:** cute, pretty, lovely, nice

**Pattern:** Common adjective. Feminine: `linda`. Very common in Latin American Spanish; in Spain `bonito/a` is more typical. Can describe people, places, or things.

**Example:** *Qué linda es tu casa.* — How lovely your house is.
