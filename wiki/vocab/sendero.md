**Meaning:** trail, path, footpath

**Pattern:** Masculine noun. `un sendero`. More rustic than `camino` (road/way) — typically used for hiking trails. Related: [[acampar]], [[aire-libre]].

**Example:** *Seguimos el sendero hasta el lago.* — We followed the trail to the lake.
