**Meaning:** cake

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: el pastel. Plural: pasteles. Also means "pastry" or "pie" in some regions.

**Example:** *Hicimos un pastel de chocolate para su cumpleaños.* — We made a chocolate cake for his birthday.
