**Meaning:** goal, target, objective (f); finish line (in a race)

**Pattern:** *alcanzar una meta* = to reach a goal; *cumplir una meta* = to achieve a goal. In sports: *cruzar la meta* = to cross the finish line.

**Example:** *Mi meta es hablar español con fluidez antes de diciembre.* — My goal is to speak Spanish fluently before December.
