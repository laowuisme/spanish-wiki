**Meaning:** ex

**Pattern:** Informal noun, invariable — `el ex` / `la ex` (short for ex-novio/ex-novia, etc.); gender shown by the article.

**Example:** *Vi a mi ex en la fiesta anoche.* — I saw my ex at the party last night.
