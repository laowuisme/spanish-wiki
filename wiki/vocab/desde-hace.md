**Meaning:** for

**Pattern:** Fixed expression: `desde hace` + [time period], paired with the present tense to describe an action that started in the past and continues now (English uses "for" + present perfect instead).

**Example:** *Vivo en Madrid desde hace tres años.* — I've lived in Madrid for three years.
