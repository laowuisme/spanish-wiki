**Meaning:** to turn on, switch on; to light (a candle/fire)

**Pattern:** Stem-changing (e→ie): *enciendo, enciendes, enciende*. Opposite of [[apagar]]. *enciende la luz* = turn on the light.

**Example:** *Enciende la luz, por favor — no veo nada.* — Turn on the light, please — I can't see anything.
