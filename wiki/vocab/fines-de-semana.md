**Meaning:** weekends (plural of `fin de semana`)

**Pattern:** `fin de semana` = weekend. The plural is `fines de semana` — `semana` does NOT take a plural -s: it stays `semana`, not `semanas`.

**Example:** *Los fines de semana salgo con amigos.* — On weekends I go out with friends.
