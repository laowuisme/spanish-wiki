**Meaning:** relieved

**Pattern:** Adjective; past participle of `aliviar`; agrees in gender/number (aliviado/aliviada); typically used with `estar`.

**Example:** *Estoy aliviado de que todo salió bien.* — I'm relieved that everything went well.
