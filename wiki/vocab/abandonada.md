**Meaning:** abandoned (feminine form)

**Pattern:** Past participle of `abandonar` used as an adjective. It agrees with the noun: `abandonado`, `abandonada`, `abandonados`, `abandonadas`. The note lists the feminine form.

**Example:** *La casa abandonada está al final de la calle.* — The abandoned house is at the end of the street.
