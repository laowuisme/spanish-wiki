**Meaning:** without a doubt, undoubtedly, certainly

**Pattern:** Fixed phrase. Often used as a sentence adverb: *Sin duda, es el mejor.* Also *sin duda alguna* for stronger emphasis.

**Example:** *Sin duda, este es el mejor restaurante de la ciudad.* — Without a doubt, this is the best restaurant in the city.
