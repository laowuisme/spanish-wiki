**Meaning:** next

**Pattern:** Fixed expression `que viene`, placed after a time noun (la semana que viene, el año que viene) — an alternative to `próximo`

**Example:** *Te veo la semana que viene.* — I'll see you next week.

Seen on 2026-10-24 as *el martes que viene* = next Tuesday (see [[martes]]): *Nos vemos el martes que viene.* — See you next Tuesday.
