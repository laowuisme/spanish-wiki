**Meaning:** to get on/in (with a sense of one's own motion) — the reflexive counterpart of [[subir]]

**Pattern:** Reflexive verb; the pronoun attaches to the infinitive (`subirme`, `subirte`, `subirse`...) unless conjugated. `subirse a + [vehicle/place]` = to get in/on.

**Example:** *Voy a subirme al coche antes de que llueva.* — I'm going to get in the car before it rains.
