**Meaning:** yes

**Pattern:** Adverb of affirmation; invariable. Opposite of `no`. Intensified in the fixed expression `sí o sí` (see [[sí-o-sí]]).

**Example:** *—¿Quieres venir? —Sí, claro.* — "Do you want to come?" "Yes, of course."
