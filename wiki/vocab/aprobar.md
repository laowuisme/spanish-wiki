**Meaning:** to pass (an exam), to approve

**Pattern:** Stem-changing verb (o→ue in present singular and 3rd plural): `apruebo, apruebas, aprueba, aprobamos, aprobáis, aprueban`.

**Example:** *¡Aprobé el examen!* — I passed the exam!
