**Meaning:** gray/white hairs; grey strands

**Pattern:** Almost always used in plural: *las canas*. Singular *la cana* = one grey hair. *tener canas* = to have grey hair; *salirle canas* = to be going grey. *peinar canas* = to be old/experienced (idiom).

**Example:** *Ya tiene algunas canas, aunque es joven.* — He already has some grey hairs, even though he's young.
