**Meaning:** to shout, to scream, to yell

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. *grito, gritas, grita, gritamos, gritan.* Often used with emotion: *gritar de alegría* (to shout with joy), *gritar de miedo* (to scream in fear).

**Example:** *No grites, por favor.* — Please don't shout.
