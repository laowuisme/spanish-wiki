**Meaning:** furthermore, in addition, also, besides

**Pattern:** Discourse connector. `además` adds a point, often with slightly more emphasis than `también`. Usually at start of a clause.

**Example:** *Es inteligente y, además, muy simpático.* — He's intelligent and, furthermore, very friendly.
