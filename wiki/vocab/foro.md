**Meaning:** forum, discussion board; (historical) forum

**Pattern:** Masculine noun. `el foro`. `un foro de debate` = a discussion forum. `publicar en un foro` = to post on a forum. Related: [[redes-sociales]].

**Example:** *Encontré la respuesta en un foro de internet.* — I found the answer on an internet forum.
