**Meaning:** a day off (from work or school)

**Pattern:** *día* is masculine → *un día libre* (not *una día libre*). *tener un día libre* = to have a day off; *tomarse un día libre* = to take a day off.

**Example:** *Hoy tengo un día libre, así que voy al parque.* — I have a day off today, so I'm going to the park.
