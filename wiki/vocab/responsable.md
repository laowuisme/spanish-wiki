**Meaning:** responsible

**Pattern:** Adjective ending in -e: same form for masc./fem., only changes for number: *responsable/responsables*. Can also function as a noun meaning "the person in charge."

**Example:** *Ella es muy responsable con su trabajo.* — She is very responsible with her work.
