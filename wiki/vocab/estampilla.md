**Meaning:** postage stamp (Latin America)

**Pattern:** Feminine noun (*la estampilla*). Used in Latin America. In Spain, *el sello* is more common. Both refer to the adhesive stamp you put on a letter or package.

**Example:** *Necesito una estampilla para esta carta.* — I need a stamp for this letter.
