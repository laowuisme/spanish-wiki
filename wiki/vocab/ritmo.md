**Meaning:** rhythm; beat; pace; tempo

**Pattern:** Masculine noun (*el ritmo*). Used in music (*ritmo de bachata*), work (*ritmo de trabajo* = work pace), and daily life (*a este ritmo* = at this rate).

**Example:** *Tiene muy buen ritmo bailando.* — She has a very good rhythm when dancing.
