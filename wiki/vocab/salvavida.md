**Meaning:** lifeguard; lifesaver (person or thing)

**Pattern:** Noun. *el/la salvavida* (invariable gender). Plural: *los salvavidas*. Also used figuratively: *eres mi salvavida* = you're my lifesaver.

**Example:** *El salvavida sacó al niño del agua.* — The lifeguard pulled the child out of the water.
