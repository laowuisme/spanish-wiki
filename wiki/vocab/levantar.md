**Meaning:** to raise, to lift; levantar el brazo = raise your arm

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. Also reflexive: *levantarse* = to get up (out of bed). Common instruction in medical and exercise contexts: *levanta el brazo* — raise your arm.

**Example:** *Levanta el brazo derecho, por favor.* — Please raise your right arm.
