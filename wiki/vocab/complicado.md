**Meaning:** complicated, complex, difficult (adj); *complicado* (m) / *complicada* (f)

**Pattern:** *es complicado* = it's complicated; *una situación complicada* = a complicated situation.

**Example:** *Esta situación es muy complicada, necesito ayuda.* — This situation is very complicated, I need help.
