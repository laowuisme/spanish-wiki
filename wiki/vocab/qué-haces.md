**Meaning:** what are you doing

**Pattern:** *¿Qué haces?* = casual way to ask what someone is doing (haces = 2nd person sg. present of *hacer*). Can also be used exclamatorily (*¡Qué haces!*) to express shock or disapproval — see [[qué-exclamations]].

**Example:** *¿Qué haces esta noche?* — What are you doing tonight?
