**Meaning:** a piece of furniture (singular); `los muebles` = furniture (collective)

**Pattern:** Rarely used in the singular for "furniture" generically — most references use the plural `los muebles`; singular `un mueble` means one specific piece.

**Example:** *Compramos un mueble nuevo para la sala.* — We bought a new piece of furniture for the living room.
