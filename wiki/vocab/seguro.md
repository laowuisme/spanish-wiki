**Meaning:** safe; sure, certain (adj); insurance (n, m)

**Pattern:** *estar seguro/a* = to be sure about something; *ser seguro* = to be safe (inherent quality). *el seguro* = insurance policy.

**Example:** *Estoy seguro de que tiene razón.* — I'm sure she's right.
