**Meaning:** on foot, walking

**Pattern:** From session pattern: *Ay, a pie no!* (Ugh, not on foot!). Contrast with transport modes: `en coche` (by car), `en autobús` (by bus), `en bici` (by bike).

**Example:** *¿Vas a pie o en autobús?* — Are you going on foot or by bus?
