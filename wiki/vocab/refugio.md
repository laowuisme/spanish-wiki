**Meaning:** shelter, refuge (m); safe haven

**Pattern:** *buscar refugio* = to seek shelter; *un refugio de montaña* = a mountain shelter

**Example:** *Buscamos refugio de la tormenta.* — We looked for shelter from the storm.
