**Meaning:** dizzy, nauseous, feeling sick

**Pattern:** Adjective from `marear` (to make dizzy/nauseous). Feminine: `mareada`. Use with `estar`: *estoy mareado* — I feel dizzy/nauseous.

**Example:** *Me siento mareado después de dar vueltas.* — I feel dizzy after spinning around.
