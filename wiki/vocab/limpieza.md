**Meaning:** cleaning; cleanliness (la limpieza, feminine)

**Pattern:** Related to the verb `limpiar` (to clean). Noun formed with `-eza` suffix, common for abstract nouns from adjectives/verbs (cf. `pobreza`, `belleza`).

**Example:** *Hago la limpieza de la casa los sábados.* — I do the house cleaning on Saturdays.
