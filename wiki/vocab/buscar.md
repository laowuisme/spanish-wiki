**Meaning:** to look for, to search for

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb (preterite: `busqué` due to c→qu spelling). `buscar` ≠ `encontrar`: buscar = to search; encontrar = to find.

**Example:** *Busco mis llaves.* — I'm looking for my keys.
