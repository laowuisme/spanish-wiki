**Meaning:** career; race (running/driving); university degree course

**Pattern:** Feminine noun (*la carrera*). Context-dependent: *una carrera profesional* = a professional career; *una carrera de coches* = a car race; *estudiar una carrera* = to study a degree.

**Example:** *Ella tiene una carrera muy exitosa.* — She has a very successful career.
