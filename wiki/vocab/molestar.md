**Meaning:** to bother; to annoy; to disturb
**Pattern:** gustar-type verb — takes indirect object pronouns: *me/te/le/nos/os/les molesta(n)*; see [[gusta-followed-by-clause]]
**Example:** *¿Qué les molesta más?* — What bothers them the most?
