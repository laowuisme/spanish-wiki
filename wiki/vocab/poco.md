**Meaning:** little, a little (quantity)

**Pattern:** `poco` vs `un poco` — both mean "a little" but `un poco` is slightly warmer/more precise. `Poco` alone can sound more emphatic/sparse. As adjective: `poco/poca/pocos/pocas` agrees with noun. As adverb (modifying verb/adj): invariable.

**Example:** *Hablo un poco de español.* — I speak a little Spanish. / *Hay poco tiempo.* — There's little time.
