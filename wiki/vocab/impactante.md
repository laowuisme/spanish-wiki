**Meaning:** striking; shocking; stunning (it has a strong effect on the viewer)

**Pattern:** One form for masculine and feminine: `un final impactante`, `una noticia impactante`. Plural: `impactantes`. The word itself does not say if the effect is good or bad. The context decides. It is related to `impacto` (impact).

**Example:** *Las fotos del huracán son impactantes.* — The photos of the hurricane are shocking.
