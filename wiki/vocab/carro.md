**Meaning:** car (Latin American Spanish); `coche` = car (Spain)

**Pattern:** Regional variation. Both mean car; use depends on dialect. `carro` is dominant in Mexico, Colombia, Venezuela; `coche` in Spain.

**Example:** *Mi carro está en el estacionamiento.* — My car is in the car park.
