**Meaning:** heating (the system that warms a house or a building)

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `la calefacción`. The plural drops the accent: `calefacciones`. Use it for the system, not for a single heater.

**Example:** *La calefacción no funciona y hace mucho frío.* — The heating does not work and it is very cold.
