**Meaning:** living room / lounge; also: hall/room (general)

**Pattern:** Rooms vocabulary. `sala de estar` = living room. Compare: `sala` = living room; `habitación`/`dormitorio`/`cuarto` = bedroom.

**Example:** *La televisión está en la sala.* — The TV is in the living room.
