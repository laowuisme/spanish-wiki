**Meaning:** wave (ocean/water wave)

**Pattern:** Feminine noun. `una ola`. Do not confuse with `hola` (hello) — common spelling error. Also: `ola de calor` = heat wave.

**Example:** *Las olas del mar son enormes hoy.* — The waves of the sea are huge today.
