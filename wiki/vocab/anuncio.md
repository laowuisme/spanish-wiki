**Meaning:** announcement; advertisement, ad

**Pattern:** Masculine noun (*el anuncio*). *Poner un anuncio* = to place an ad. *Anuncio de trabajo* = job listing. The verb form is *anunciar* (to announce).

**Example:** *Vi tu anuncio en internet.* — I saw your ad online.
