**Meaning:** dishwasher (appliance) / kitchen sink (in some Latin American countries)

**Pattern:** Compound noun: `lava` (washes) + `platos` (plates). Masculine: `el lavaplatos`. In Spain also called `el lavavajillas`.

**Example:** *Pon los platos en el lavaplatos.* — Put the plates in the dishwasher.
