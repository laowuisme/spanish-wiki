**Meaning:** in love; enamoured

**Pattern:** Adjective with gender agreement: *enamorado* (masc.) / *enamorada* (fem.). Used with *estar* (not ser): *estar enamorado de* = to be in love with. The verb *enamorarse* = to fall in love.

**Example:** *Estoy enamorado de ella.* — I am in love with her.
