**Meaning:** salt

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `la sal` — regular, since the stress does not fall on the initial `a` (contrast [[agua]], which is feminine but takes `el agua`).

**Example:** *Pásame la sal, por favor.* — Pass me the salt, please.
