**Meaning:** to argue; to discuss (a topic)

**Pattern:** Regular -ir verb. *discuten* = they argue; *discutiendo* = arguing. False cognate: in Spanish *discutir* usually means to argue, not just to discuss.

**Example:** *Están discutiendo por el dinero otra vez.* — They're arguing about money again.
