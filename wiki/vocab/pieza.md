**Meaning:** piece, part; in parts of Latin America, a room

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `una pieza de repuesto` (spare part), `una pieza de ajedrez` (chess piece). In Chile `pieza` also means a room. The note lists it with housing words and gives no sentence, so the meaning is not known.

**Example:** *Necesito una pieza de repuesto para la lavadora.* — I need a spare part for the washing machine.
