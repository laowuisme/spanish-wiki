**Meaning:** room / bedroom (informal); also "quarter" (fraction or quarter past the hour)

**Pattern:** Rooms vocabulary. Informal synonym for `habitación`/`dormitorio`. Also appears in time expressions: `las tres y cuarto` (quarter past three).

**Example:** *Mi cuarto es pequeño.* — My room is small.
