**Meaning:** hair (on head or body); also: fur (animals)

**Pattern:** Body vocabulary. `pelo largo VS corto` = long VS short hair.

**Example:** *Tiene el pelo largo y rubio.* — She has long blonde hair.

Seen again on 2026-10-24 with the hair-description words [[castaño]] and [[rizado]]: *pelo castaño*, *pelo rizado*. Do not confuse it with [[piel]] (skin). See [[forgets-pelo]].
