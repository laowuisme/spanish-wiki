**Meaning:** bald

**Pattern:** Adjective with gender agreement: *calvo* (masc.) / *calva* (fem.). Related noun: *la calvicie* (baldness). *Quedarse calvo* = to go bald.

**Example:** *Mi abuelo es completamente calvo.* — My grandfather is completely bald.
