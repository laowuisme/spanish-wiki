**Meaning:** cucumber

**Pattern:** Masculine noun. `un pepino`. Colloquial: `me importa un pepino` = I don't care at all (I couldn't care less).

**Example:** *La ensalada lleva pepino y tomate.* — The salad has cucumber and tomato.
