**Meaning:** maybe, perhaps

**Pattern:** Can be followed by indicative or subjunctive. With subjunctive = more uncertain; with indicative = slightly more certain. In practice, indicative is common in speech.

**Example:** *Tal vez voy mañana.* — Maybe I'll go tomorrow. (indicative, informal)
