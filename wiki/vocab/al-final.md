**Meaning:** in the end, finally (standalone) — contrast [[al-final-de]], which requires a following noun ("at the end of X")

**Pattern:** Used alone at the start or end of a sentence to summarize an outcome, not to locate something physically.

**Example:** *Al final, decidimos quedarnos en casa.* — In the end, we decided to stay home.
