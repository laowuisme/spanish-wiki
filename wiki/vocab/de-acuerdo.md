**Meaning:** agreed, OK, alright, in agreement

**Pattern:** Fixed phrase. Used as a standalone response (*¡De acuerdo!* = OK/Agreed!) or with *estar*: *estar de acuerdo con* = to agree with someone/something. Compare: *de acuerdo* (agreed) vs *de acuerdo con* (according to / in accordance with).

**Example:** *¿Nos vemos a las siete? — De acuerdo.* — Shall we meet at seven? — Agreed.
