**Meaning:** since, from (a point in time or place)

**Pattern:** Preposition. `desde + time` = since. `desde... hasta...` = from... to... (time or place).

**Example:** *Vivo aquí desde 2020.* — I've lived here since 2020. / *desde la mañana* — since the morning.
