**Meaning:** stove, cooker (Latin America) / heater, space heater (Spain)

**Pattern:** Regional split: in most of Latin America `estufa` = kitchen stove/oven unit. In Spain it typically means a heater. Context usually makes meaning clear.

**Example:** *El agua está hirviendo en la estufa.* — The water is boiling on the stove.
