**Meaning:** see you, see you around, catch you later

**Pattern:** Lit. "we see each other." Common informal farewell. `¡Nos vemos!` = See you! `Nos vemos mañana` = See you tomorrow. Other farewells: [[chao]], `hasta luego`, `hasta pronto`.

**Example:** *¡Nos vemos el viernes!* — See you Friday!
