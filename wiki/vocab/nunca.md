**Meaning:** never

**Pattern:** Negative adverb. After the verb, requires `no` before the verb (*No como nunca carne* = I never eat meat); before the verb, `no` is omitted (*Nunca como carne*).

**Example:** *Nunca voy al gimnasio.* — I never go to the gym.
