**Meaning:** piece of advice; (plural) advice (el consejo, masculine)

**Pattern:** Often used as *dar un consejo* (to give advice) or *pedir consejo* (to ask for advice). Not countable the same way in English — "advice" has no plural, but Spanish `consejos` does.

**Example:** *Mi abuela siempre me da buenos consejos.* — My grandmother always gives me good advice.
