**Meaning:** entertaining, enjoyable, fun (of an activity, film, book)

**Pattern:** Adjective with gender agreement: *entretenido* (masc.) / *entretenida* (fem.). From the verb *entretener* (to entertain). Not the same as *divertido* — *entretenido* often implies "keeps you occupied"; *divertido* = fun/amusing.

**Example:** *La película es muy entretenida.* — The film is very entertaining.
