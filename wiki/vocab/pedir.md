**Meaning:** to ask for, to request, to order (food)

**Pattern:** Stem-changing verb (e→i, present): *pido, pides, pide, pedimos, pedís, piden*. Preterite (3rd person only): *pedí, pediste, **pidió**, pedimos, pedisteis, **pidieron*** — see [[preterite-irregular-verbs]]. Participle: `pedido` — see [[present-perfect]].

**Example:** *Pidió una pizza.* — He/she ordered a pizza.
