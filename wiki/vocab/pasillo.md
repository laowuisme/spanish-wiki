**Meaning:** aisle (supermarket); corridor, hallway

**Pattern:** Masculine noun (*el pasillo*). In a supermarket: *el pasillo de los lácteos* = the dairy aisle. In a building: corridor/hallway. In transport (plane, cinema): aisle seat = *asiento de pasillo*.

**Example:** *El azúcar está en el pasillo tres.* — The sugar is in aisle three.
