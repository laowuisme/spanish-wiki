**Meaning:** to come

**Pattern:** Irregular verb. Key forms: `vengo` (yo), `vienes` (tú), `viene` (él/ella), `venimos` (nosotros), `venís` (vosotros), `vienen` (ellos). Contrast with `ir` (to go): `venir` = coming toward speaker; `ir` = going away. Preterite (j-stem): *vine, viniste, vino, vinimos, vinisteis, vinieron* — see [[preterite-irregular-verbs]].

**Example:** *¿Vienes a la fiesta?* — Are you coming to the party?
