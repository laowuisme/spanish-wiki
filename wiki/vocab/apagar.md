**Meaning:** to turn off, to switch off, to put out (a fire/light)

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb with spelling change in yo: *apago* (I turn off). Opposite: *encender* (to turn on). Used for lights, devices, fires: *apagar la luz* (turn off the light), *apagar el móvil* (turn off the phone).

**Example:** *Apago la luz antes de dormir.* — I turn off the light before sleeping.
