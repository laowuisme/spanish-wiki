**Meaning:** (I)  want (you), (I)  love (you)

**Pattern:** Fixed expression `te quiero` — first-person `querer` + `te` (you); everyday way to say "I love you" to a partner, family, or close friend. See [[te-quiero-vs-te-amo]].

**Example:** *Te quiero mucho.* — I love you very much.
