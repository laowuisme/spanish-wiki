**Meaning:** scarf

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el pañuelo`. Can mean handkerchief, bandana, or headscarf depending on context.

**Example:** *Lleva un pañuelo rojo en el cuello.* — She's wearing a red scarf around her neck.
