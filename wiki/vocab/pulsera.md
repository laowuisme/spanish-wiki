**Meaning:** bracelet; wristband

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `una pulsera`. Also used for a wristband (an event wristband) and `pulsera de actividad` (fitness tracker).

**Example:** *Me regaló una pulsera de plata.* — She gave me a silver bracelet.
