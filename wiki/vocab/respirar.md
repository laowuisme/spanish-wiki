**Meaning:** to breathe

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. Common in medical and mindfulness contexts. *respira hondo* = breathe deeply; *respira despacio* = breathe slowly.

**Example:** *Respira hondo y relájate.* — Breathe deeply and relax.
