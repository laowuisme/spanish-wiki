**Meaning:** badly

**Pattern:** Adverb, invariable: modifies verbs, e.g. *cantar mal* (to sing badly). Also the shortened form of *malo* before masculine singular nouns (*mal día*).

**Example:** *Canté muy mal en el concierto.* — I sang very badly at the concert.
