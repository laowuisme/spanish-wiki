**Meaning:** form (document to fill in)

**Pattern:** Noun, masculine: *el formulario*. *rellenar un formulario* = to fill in a form. Common in medical, administrative, and banking contexts.

**Example:** *Por favor, rellene este formulario antes de ver al médico.* — Please fill in this form before seeing the doctor.
