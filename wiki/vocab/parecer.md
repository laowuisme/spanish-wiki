**Meaning:** to seem, to appear, to look like

**Pattern:** Irregular yo form: *parezco* (like *conocer → conozco*). Key forms: *parece* (it seems/looks), *me parece* (it seems to me / I think). *¿Qué te parece?* = What do you think? *Parece que* + indicative = It seems that...

**Example:** *Parece que va a llover.* — It looks like it's going to rain.
