**Meaning:** chestnut brown, brown (hair and eyes)

**Pattern:** Colour adjective that agrees with the noun: `pelo castaño`, `ojos castaños`, `melena castaña`. Compare [[rubio]] (blond) and [[moreno]] (dark-haired). `castaña` is also the chestnut itself. See [[pelo]].

**Example:** *Tiene el pelo castaño y los ojos verdes.* — She has brown hair and green eyes.
