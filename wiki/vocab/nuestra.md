**Meaning:** our, ours

**Pattern:** Feminine singular possessive adjective; agrees with the noun possessed, not the possessor: *nuestra casa* (our house). Masculine form: `nuestro`.

**Example:** *Esta es nuestra casa.* — This is our house.
