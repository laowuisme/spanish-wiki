**Meaning:** suggestion

**Pattern:** Commonly paired with `hacer una sugerencia` (to make a suggestion) or `tener una sugerencia` (to have a suggestion).

**Example:** *Tengo una sugerencia para la fiesta.* — I have a suggestion for the party.
