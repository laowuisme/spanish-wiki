**Meaning:** finally, lastly (sequencing a list of points, not "in the end" — contrast [[al-final]])

**Pattern:** Fixed discourse marker used to introduce the final item in a spoken or written sequence.

**Example:** *Por último, quiero darle las gracias a todos.* — Finally, I want to thank everyone.
