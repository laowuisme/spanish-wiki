**Meaning:** complaint, grievance

**Pattern:** Feminine noun (*la queja*). From the verb *quejarse* (to complain) — a reflexive verb. See [[reflexive-verbs]]. *Presentar una queja* = to file a complaint.

**Clarification — se queja vs quejarse:**
- *quejarse* = the infinitive: "to complain" — used after modal verbs (*suele quejarse* = she tends to complain)
- *se queja* = present tense, 3rd person singular: "he/she complains" — conjugated form with reflexive pronoun attached
- The reflexive pronoun (*me, te, se, nos, os, se*) changes with the subject; the pronoun always stays with the verb.
> *Ella se queja mucho.* — She complains a lot. (*se queja* = conjugated)
> *No puede dejar de quejarse.* — She can't stop complaining. (*quejarse* = infinitive)

**Example:** *Tengo una queja sobre el servicio.* — I have a complaint about the service.
