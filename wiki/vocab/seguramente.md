**Meaning:** surely, certainly; probably, most likely

**Pattern:** Derived from `seguro` (sure/certain). Context determines meaning: high confidence → "surely"; uncertainty → "probably". `seguramente va a llover` = it will probably rain. Synonym: `probablemente`.

**Example:** *Seguramente llega tarde.* — He'll probably arrive late. / He'll surely arrive late.
