**Meaning:** video call

**Pattern:** Feminine noun. `hacer una videollamada` = to make a video call. `estar en videollamada` = to be on a video call. Related: [[tecnologia]].

**Example:** *Hacemos una videollamada con la familia cada domingo.* — We make a video call with the family every Sunday.
