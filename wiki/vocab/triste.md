**Meaning:** sad

**Pattern:** Adjective ending in `-e`; invariable for gender, only changes for number: `triste/tristes`; used with `estar` for a temporary emotion, `ser` for a sad thing/situation.

**Example:** *Estoy triste porque llueve.* — I'm sad because it's raining.
