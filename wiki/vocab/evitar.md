**Meaning:** to avoid

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. *evitar + infinitive* = to avoid doing something. *evitar + noun* = to avoid something. Compare: *evitar* (to avoid/prevent) vs *escaparse* (to escape/slip away).

**Example:** *Hay que evitar el alcohol cuando tomas antibióticos.* — You should avoid alcohol when you're taking antibiotics.
