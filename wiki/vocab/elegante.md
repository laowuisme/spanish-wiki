**Meaning:** elegant

**Pattern:** Invariable for gender (same form m/f): *el hombre elegante*, *la mujer elegante*. Only changes for number: elegante/elegantes.

**Example:** *Lleva un vestido muy elegante.* — She's wearing a very elegant dress.
