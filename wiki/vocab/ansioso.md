**Meaning:** anxious, eager

**Pattern:** Adjective; agrees in gender/number: `ansioso/ansiosa`. Often followed by `por` + noun/infinitive.

**Example:** *Estoy ansioso por el examen de mañana.* — I'm anxious about tomorrow's exam.
