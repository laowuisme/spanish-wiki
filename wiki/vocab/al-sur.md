**Meaning:** to the south / in the south

**Pattern:** Directional phrases using cardinal points: `al norte` (north), `al sur` (south), `al este` (east), `al oeste` (west). `al` = a + el (contraction). See [[al-usage]].

**Example:** *Viajamos al sur del país.* — We travelled to the south of the country.
