**Meaning:** to serve

**Pattern:** Stem-changing verb (e→i, present): *sirvo, sirves, sirve, servimos, servís, sirven*. Preterite (3rd person only): *serví, serviste, **sirvió**, servimos, servisteis, **sirvieron*** — see [[preterite-irregular-verbs]].

**Example:** *La camarera sirvió la comida.* — The waitress served the food.
