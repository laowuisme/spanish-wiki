**Meaning:** granddaughter

**Pattern:** Feminine noun (*la nieta*). Masculine: *el nieto* (grandson). Plural: *los nietos* (grandchildren, mixed or all-male). Related: *los abuelos* (grandparents), *el abuelo/la abuela*.

**Example:** *Mi abuela tiene tres nietas.* — My grandmother has three granddaughters.
