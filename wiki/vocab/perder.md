**Meaning:** to lose; also: to miss (a bus/train)

**Pattern:** Stem-changing verb (e→ie): `pierdo, pierdes, pierde, perdemos, perdéis, pierden`. `perdida` (fem adj) = lost.

**Example:** *¡Perdí las llaves!* — I lost my keys! / *Perdiste el tren.* — You missed the train.
