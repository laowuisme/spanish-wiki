**Meaning:** behind

**Pattern:** Use `detrás de` + noun for "behind something": `detrás de la puerta`. Without `de` it stands alone: `Mira detrás.` Its opposite is `delante`. `atrás` (back, backwards) is a different word.

**Example:** *El perro está detrás de la puerta.* — The dog is behind the door.
