**Meaning:** backpacker (a traveler who carries a backpack and usually has a small budget)

**Pattern:** Masculine `el mochilero`, feminine `la mochilera`. It comes from [[mochila]] (backpack). The ending `-ero` names a person linked to a thing, as in [[cartero]] (mail carrier).

**Example:** *Los mochileros duermen en hostales baratos.* — Backpackers sleep in cheap hostels.
