**Meaning:** to hand in, submit, deliver; to hand over

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. *entrego, entregas, entrega*; *entregar la tarea* = to hand in homework; *entregar un paquete* = to deliver a package.

**Example:** *Entregué el informe ayer por la tarde.* — I handed in the report yesterday afternoon.
