**Meaning:** tool

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `la herramienta`. A toolbox is `la caja de herramientas`. A specific tool from the same note: [[destornillador]] (screwdriver).

**Example:** *Mi padre guarda las herramientas en el garaje.* — My father keeps the tools in the garage.
