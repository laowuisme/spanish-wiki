**Meaning:** raincoat (m); waterproof (adj)

**Pattern:** Same form as adjective: *una tela impermeable* = a waterproof fabric; *llevar el impermeable* = to wear the raincoat

**Example:** *No me mojé porque llevaba impermeable.* — I didn't get wet because I was wearing a raincoat.
