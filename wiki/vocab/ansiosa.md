**Meaning:** anxious, nervous, worried (feminine form; masculine: ansioso)

**Pattern:** `estar + ansiosa/ansioso` — describes a temporary emotional state, so always uses [[estar]], not ser.

**Example:** *Estoy ansiosa por el torneo.* — I'm anxious about the tournament.
