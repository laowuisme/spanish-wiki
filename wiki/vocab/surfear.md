**Meaning:** to surf

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. Anglicism widely used in Latin America. Also `hacer surf` / `practicar surf`. `surfear en internet` = to surf the internet (though `navegar` is more standard online).

**Example:** *Quiero aprender a surfear este verano.* — I want to learn to surf this summer.
