**Meaning:** super

**Pattern:** Colloquial intensifier; as an adverb it modifies adjectives (*súper bien* = really well/great), and as an invariable adjective it can describe something directly (*es un plan súper* = it's a great plan). Similar in function to `muy` but more informal.

**Example:** *La película estuvo súper interesante.* — The movie was super interesting.
