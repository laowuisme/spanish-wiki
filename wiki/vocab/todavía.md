**Meaning:** still, yet

**Pattern:** Used like English "still" (positive: `todavía + present`) or "yet" (negative: `todavía no`).

**Example:** *Todavía no hablo bien.* — I still don't speak well. / *¿Todavía estás aquí?* — Are you still here?
