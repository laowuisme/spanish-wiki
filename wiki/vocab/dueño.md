**Meaning:** owner (male)

**Pattern:** Masculine `el dueño`, feminine `la dueña`. The owned thing follows `de`: `el dueño de la casa`. [[propietario]] is a near synonym.

**Example:** *El dueño de la casa vive en otra ciudad.* — The owner of the house lives in another city.
