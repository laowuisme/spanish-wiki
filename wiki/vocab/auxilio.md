**Meaning:** help, aid (used in emergencies: ¡Auxilio! = Help!)

**Pattern:** Emergency/urgent vocabulary. `¡Auxilio!` = shout for help. Different from `ayudar` (to help, verb) and `ayuda` (help, general).

**Example:** *¡Auxilio! ¡Llamen a la policía!* — Help! Call the police!
