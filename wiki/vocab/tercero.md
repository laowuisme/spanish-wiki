**Meaning:** third

**Pattern:** Ordinal number. Before a masculine singular noun it loses the -o: `el tercer piso` (see [[piso]]). The feminine is `tercera`: `la tercera vez`. The note lists the form `tercera`.

**Example:** *Es la tercera vez que llamo hoy.* — It is the third time I call today.
