**Meaning:** round trip (there and back)

**Pattern:** Fixed travel phrase. `Un billete de ida y vuelta` = a return ticket. `De ida` alone = one-way.

**Example:** *Quiero un billete de ida y vuelta a Madrid.* — I'd like a round-trip ticket to Madrid.
