**Meaning:** housing; a home, a dwelling

**Pattern:** Feminine noun. A general word for any place to live (a house or a flat), wider than [[casa]]. Typical uses: `alquilar una vivienda`, `el precio de la vivienda`. See [[alquiler]] and [[piso]].

**Example:** *El precio de la vivienda ha subido mucho en esta ciudad.* — Housing prices have gone up a lot in this city.
