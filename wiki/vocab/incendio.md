**Meaning:** fire (a large, destructive fire; a blaze)

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el incendio`. It names a fire that burns a building or a forest. For a flame or fire in general, Spanish uses `fuego` (not yet created).

**Example:** *El incendio destruyó la casa abandonada.* — The fire destroyed the abandoned house. (see [[abandonada]])
