**Meaning:** bye (informal)

**Pattern:** Informal farewell, borrowed from Italian *ciao*. Also spelled *chau*. Common across Latin America and informal Spanish. Equivalent to *adiós* (formal) or *hasta luego* (see you later).

**Example:** *Chao, nos vemos mañana.* — Bye, see you tomorrow.
