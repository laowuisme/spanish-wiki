**Meaning:** better, best

**Pattern:** Comparative/superlative of `bueno` (good). Irregular: `bueno → mejor` (no `más bueno` in standard). Opposite: `peor` (worse).

**Example:** *Hoy me siento mejor.* — Today I feel better.
