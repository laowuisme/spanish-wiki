**Meaning:** the reverse/wrong side of something; also "setback" (figurative)

**Pattern:** Most frequently seen in the fixed phrase `al revés` (backwards, inside out, the other way around).

**Example:** *Llevas la camisa al revés.* — You have your shirt on backwards.
