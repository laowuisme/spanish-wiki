**Meaning:** heavy

**Pattern:** Adjective — agrees in gender/number: `pesado/pesada/pesados/pesadas`. Colloquially also means "annoying/tiresome."

**Example:** *Esta maleta es muy pesada.* — This suitcase is very heavy.
