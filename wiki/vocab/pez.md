**Meaning:** fish

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el pez` — plural `los peces`. Refers to a live fish, as opposed to `pescado` (fish as food).

**Example:** *Tengo un pez dorado en mi acuario.* — I have a goldfish in my aquarium.
