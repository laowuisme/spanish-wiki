**Meaning:** to improve, to get better

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. Used both transitively (*mejorar algo* = to improve something) and intransitively (*estoy mejorando* = I'm getting better). Common in illness contexts: *¡Que te mejores!* — Get well soon!

**Example:** *Necesito mejorar mi español.* — I need to improve my Spanish.
