**Meaning:** to water (plants)

**Pattern:** Irregular e→ie stem-changing -ar verb (*riego, riegas, riega, regamos, regáis, riegan*), same pattern as *pensar/cerrar*. Preterite yo-form has the -gar spelling change → *regué* (see [[conjugation-spelling-patterns]] and [[regué]]).

**Example:** *Riego el jardín todos los días.* — I water the garden every day.
