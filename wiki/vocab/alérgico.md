**Meaning:** allergic

**Pattern:** Adjective; agrees in gender/number: `alérgico/alérgica`. Usually followed by `a` + the allergen: *alérgico a los frutos secos*.

**Example:** *Soy alérgico al polen.* — I'm allergic to pollen.
