**Meaning:** to go up, rise, climb; to upload; to get on (transport)

**Pattern:** Regular -ir verb. *subo, subes, sube*; *subiendo* = going up (gerund). *subir al autobús* = to get on the bus.

**Example:** *Estamos subiendo la montaña lentamente.* — We're climbing the mountain slowly.
