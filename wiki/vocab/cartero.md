**Meaning:** postman, mail carrier

**Pattern:** Masculine/feminine noun: *el cartero* / *la cartera*. Note: *la cartera* also means wallet/bag — context usually clarifies. *El cartero* delivers mail.

**Example:** *El cartero trajo un paquete esta mañana.* — The postman brought a package this morning.
