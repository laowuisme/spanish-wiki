**Meaning:** known, well-known, familiar; as a noun, *un conocido / una conocida* = an acquaintance

**Pattern:** Past participle of *conocer* (to know/meet), used adjectivally. *Ser conocido* (to be known/famous) uses **ser** for an inherent trait — see [[ser-vs-estar]].

**Example:** *Es un actor muy conocido.* — He's a very well-known actor.
