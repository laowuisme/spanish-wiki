**Meaning:** to check, to review, to revise; regular -ar verb

**Pattern:** `revisar + noun` — e.g., revisar el correo (check email), revisar los apuntes (review notes). Not the same as [[repasar]] (to go over material for study).

**Example:** *Siempre reviso el teléfono antes de dormir.* — I always check my phone before sleeping.
