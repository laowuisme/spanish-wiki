**Meaning:** to pick up, pick up

**Pattern:** -er verb with a spelling change g→j before *a/o* (*yo recojo*) to preserve the soft g sound; otherwise regular.

**Example:** *Voy a recoger a mi hijo del colegio.* — I'm going to pick up my son from school.
