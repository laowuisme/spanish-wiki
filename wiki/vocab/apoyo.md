**Meaning:** support, backing, help (m)

**Pattern:** *apoyar* = to support (verb); *con tu apoyo* = with your support; *el apoyo emocional* = emotional support.

**Example:** *Gracias por tu apoyo durante esta situación difícil.* — Thank you for your support during this difficult situation.
