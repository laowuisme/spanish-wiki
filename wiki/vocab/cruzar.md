**Meaning:** to cross

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb, but as a `-zar` verb the `z` changes to `c` before `-e` endings — preterite yo-form (`crucé`) and every subjunctive/command form (`cruce`, `cruces`...). See [[conjugation-spelling-patterns]] for the full rule, and [[cruce-vs-cruze]] for the specific formal-command mistake this causes.

**Example:** *Cruce la calle y camine todo recto.* — Cross the street and walk straight ahead. (formal/usted command)
