**Meaning:** dark (lacking light); dark (colour); obscure

**Pattern:** `oscuro/a`. `está oscuro` = it's dark. `a oscuras` = in the dark (without light). Opposite: `claro` (light/bright).

**Example:** *El cuarto está muy oscuro por la noche.* — The room is very dark at night.
