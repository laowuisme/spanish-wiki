**Meaning:** area

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `la zona`. Common in compounds: `zona horaria` (time zone), `zona de descanso` (rest area).

**Example:** *Esta zona de la ciudad es muy tranquila.* — This area of the city is very quiet.
