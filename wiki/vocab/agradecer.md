**Meaning:** to thank; to be grateful for; to appreciate

**Pattern:** Irregular yo form: *agradezco* (I thank/appreciate). Like *conocer* → *conozco*. Regular elsewhere: *agradeces, agradece*, etc. More formal than *dar las gracias*. *Te lo agradezco* = I appreciate it (literally: I thank you for it).

**Example:** *Agradezco tu ayuda.* — I appreciate your help.
