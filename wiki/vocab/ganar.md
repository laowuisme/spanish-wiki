**Meaning:** to win; to earn (money); regular -ar verb

**Pattern:** `ganar + direct object` for both senses: ganar el partido (win the match), ganar dinero (earn money). Contrast with [[perder]] (to lose).

**Example:** *¡Ganamos el torneo!* — We won the tournament!
