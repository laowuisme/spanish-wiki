**Meaning:** emergency room, A&E (Accident & Emergency)

**Pattern:** Noun phrase. *la sala de urgencias* (f). Synonyms: *urgencias* (A&E department, colloquial shorthand), *sala de emergencias* (Latin America). Common in medical contexts.

**Example:** *Fue a la sala de urgencias con fiebre alta.* — She went to the emergency room with a high fever.
