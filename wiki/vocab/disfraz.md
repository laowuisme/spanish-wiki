**Meaning:** costume; disguise

**Pattern:** Masculine noun. The plural is `disfraces`: z becomes c before e, the same spelling change as in -zar verbs (see [[conjugation-spelling-patterns]]). The verb is `disfrazarse de` (to dress up as).

**Example:** *Para la fiesta me puse un disfraz de pirata.* — For the party I wore a pirate costume.
