**Meaning:** spoon (cucharas = spoons)

**Pattern:** Utensils vocabulary. `cuchara` (spoon), `cucharita` (teaspoon). Also see `cubiertos` (cutlery set).

**Example:** *Necesito una cuchara para la sopa.* — I need a spoon for the soup.
