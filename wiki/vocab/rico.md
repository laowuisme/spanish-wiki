**Meaning:** tasty

**Pattern:** Adjective; agrees in gender/number: *rico/rica/ricos/ricas*. Means "tasty/delicious" with food (*¡Qué rico!*) or "rich" (wealthy), depending on context.

**Example:** *Esta sopa está muy rica.* — This soup is very tasty.
