**Meaning:** vacuum cleaner

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `la aspiradora`. Used with `pasar` — `pasar la aspiradora` = to vacuum (literally "to pass the vacuum cleaner"). Do not say *aspirar el piso* in most registers.

**Example:** *Voy a pasar la aspiradora en el salón.* — I'm going to vacuum the living room.
