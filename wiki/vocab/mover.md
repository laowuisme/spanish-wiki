**Meaning:** to move (something); to shift

**Pattern:** Stem-changing verb o→ue: *muevo, mueves, mueve, movemos, movéis, mueven*. Reflexive *moverse* = to move (oneself), to get around. Note: to move house = *mudarse*, not mover.

**Example:** *No puedo mover el brazo.* — I can't move my arm.
