**Meaning:** to rent

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb; `alquilar un apartamento` works for both renting from someone and renting out to someone — context (subject/object) determines direction, similar to English "rent."

**Example:** *Decidimos alquilar un apartamento más pequeño.* — We decided to rent a smaller apartment.
