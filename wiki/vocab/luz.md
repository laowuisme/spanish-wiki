**Meaning:** light; electricity

**Pattern:** Feminine noun (*la luz*). Plural: *las luces*. Common uses: *la luz del sol* (sunlight), *apagar/encender la luz* (turn off/on the light), *la luz* can also refer to electricity (*pagar la luz* = to pay the electricity bill).

**Example:** *Enciende la luz, por favor.* — Turn on the light, please.
