**Meaning:** to call (phone); to call/name; `llamarse` = to be called/named

**Pattern:** Two main uses: `llamar a alguien` = to call someone (phone); `llamarse` = to be named. `Me llamo...` = My name is... (literally: I call myself).

**Example:** *Te llamo esta noche.* — I'll call you tonight. / *¿Cómo te llamas?* — What's your name?
