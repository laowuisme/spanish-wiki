**Meaning:** to act (in a film/play); to behave; to take action

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb with spelling change: *actú-o, actú-as, actú-a* (accent on u in stressed syllables in present tense). Actor/actriz are the related nouns.

**Example:** *Ella actúa muy bien en esa película.* — She acts very well in that film.
