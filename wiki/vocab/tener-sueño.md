**Meaning:** to be sleepy, be sleepy

**Pattern:** `tener sueño` — tener expression; conjugate `tener` to match the subject. See [[tener-expressions]].

**Example:** *Tengo sueño, me voy a dormir.* — I'm sleepy, I'm going to bed.
