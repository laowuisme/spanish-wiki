**Meaning:** I know (a person/place) — first person singular of `conocer`

**Pattern:** `conocer` = to know (people/places, through familiarity). Distinct from `saber` (to know facts/how to). First person singular is irregular: `conozco`. `conoce` = he/she knows.

**Example:** *Conozco a tu esposo.* — I know your husband. / *¿Conoces Madrid?* — Do you know Madrid?
