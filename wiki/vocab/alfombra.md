**Meaning:** carpet, rug

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `la alfombra`. Can refer to a large fitted carpet or a small decorative rug. Related: `pasar la aspiradora` (to vacuum), `la moqueta` (wall-to-wall carpet, Spain).

**Example:** *Hay una alfombra grande en el salón.* — There's a big rug in the living room.
