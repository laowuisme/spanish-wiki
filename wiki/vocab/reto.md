**Meaning:** challenge

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el reto`. The spelling is the same as `reto` (I challenge), the first person of `retar`. An article before it (`un reto`, `el reto`) shows that it is the noun.

**Example:** *Aprender español es un reto para mí.* — Learning Spanish is a challenge for me.
