**Meaning:** surname, last name, family name

**Pattern:** Masculine noun. `el apellido`. In Spanish-speaking countries, people typically have two surnames: `primer apellido` (father's) + `segundo apellido` (mother's). `nombre y apellido` = full name.

**Example:** *¿Cuál es tu apellido?* — What is your last name?
