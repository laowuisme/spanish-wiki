**Meaning:** on the outskirts, in the suburbs

**Pattern:** `las afueras` is a feminine plural noun. Use `en las afueras de` + the city: `vive en las afueras de Madrid`. Do not confuse it with `fuera` (outside).

**Example:** *Vivimos en las afueras de la ciudad, cerca del bosque.* — We live on the outskirts of the city, near the forest.
