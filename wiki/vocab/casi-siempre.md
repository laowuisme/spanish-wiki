**Meaning:** almost always; contrasts with siempre (always) and a veces (sometimes)

**Pattern:** Frequency adverb — placed before the verb or at the start of the sentence.

**Example:** *Casi siempre desayuno antes de las ocho.* — I almost always have breakfast before eight.
