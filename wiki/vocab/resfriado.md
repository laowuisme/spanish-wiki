**Meaning:** cold (illness), the common cold

**Pattern:** Health vocabulary. `tener un resfriado` = to have a cold. `estar resfriado` = to be down with a cold.

**Example:** *Tengo un resfriado y no puedo salir.* — I have a cold and can't go out.
