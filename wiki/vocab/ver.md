**Meaning:** to see, to watch

**Pattern:** Irregular -er verb: *veo, ves, ve, vemos, veis, ven*. Reflexive form *verse* = to look/seem — see [[se-ve-construction]]. Preterite: *vi, viste, vio, vimos, visteis, vieron* (regular endings, no accents). Imperfect (one of only 3 irregular verbs): *veía, veías, veía, veíamos, veíais, veían* — see [[past-tense]], [[preterite-irregular-verbs]].

**Example:** *Quiero ver una película.* — I want to watch a movie.
