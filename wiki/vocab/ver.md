**Meaning:** to see, to watch

**Pattern:** Irregular -er verb: *veo, ves, ve, vemos, veis, ven*. Reflexive form *verse* = to look/seem — see [[se-ve-construction]].

**Example:** *Quiero ver una película.* — I want to watch a movie.
