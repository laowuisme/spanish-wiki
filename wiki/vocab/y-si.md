**Meaning:** ¿Y si...? = what if...?; used to propose a hypothetical or make a suggestion

**Pattern:** *¿Y si + present indicative?* → suggestion: *¿Y si vamos al cine?* = What if we go to the cinema? / How about we go to the cinema? More tentative than direct proposals.

**Example:** *¿Y si pedimos una pizza en vez de cocinar?* — What if we order pizza instead of cooking?
