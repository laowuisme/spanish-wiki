**Meaning:** demanding, exacting, strict

**Pattern:** Invariable in gender — same form for masculine and feminine (*un jefe exigente* / *una profesora exigente*). From *exigir* (to demand/require).

**Example:** *Es una persona muy exigente.* — She is a very demanding person.
