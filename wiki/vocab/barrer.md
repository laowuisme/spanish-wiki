**Meaning:** to sweep

**Pattern:** Regular -er verb. Common phrase: `barrer el piso` (to sweep the floor). Pair with `escoba` (broom) or `limpiar` for broader cleaning.

**Example:** *Hay que barrer el piso antes de fregar.* — You need to sweep the floor before mopping.
