**Meaning:** interview

**Pattern:** Feminine noun (*la entrevista*). *Hacer una entrevista* = to conduct/do an interview. *Tener una entrevista* = to have an interview. The verb form is *entrevistar* (to interview).

**Example:** *Tengo una entrevista de trabajo mañana.* — I have a job interview tomorrow.
