**Meaning:** leave

**Pattern:** Fixed expression `salir de` + [place]: the verb `salir` ('to go out/leave') followed by `de` to specify the place being left.

**Example:** *Voy a salir de casa a las ocho.* — I'm going to leave home at eight.
