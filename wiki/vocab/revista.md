**Meaning:** magazine; journal; review

**Pattern:** Feminine noun (*la revista*). *Las revistas* = magazines (plural). Not to confuse with *la reseña* (review/critique) or *el periódico/diario* (newspaper).

**Example:** *Compré una revista de cocina.* — I bought a cooking magazine.
