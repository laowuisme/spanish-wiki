**Meaning:** to read, read

**Pattern:** Regular *-er* verb in the present; irregular spelling in the preterite/gerund (i→y between vowels): *leyó, leyeron, leyendo*.

**Example:** *Leo un libro todas las noches.* — I read a book every night.
