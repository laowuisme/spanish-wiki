**Meaning:** frightening, dreadful, appalling

**Pattern:** Regular -oso adjective (espantoso/espantosa). Common as the complement of [[parecer]]. The word keeps coming back — see [[forgets-espantoso]].

**Example:** *Te parece espantoso este color.* — This color seems dreadful to you.
