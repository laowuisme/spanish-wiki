**Meaning:** to walk; to go around; to be (doing something); to work (of a machine)

**Pattern:** Irregular preterite: `anduve`, `anduviste`, `anduvo`. Present is regular: `ando`, `andas`, `anda`. `andar` + gerund = to be going around doing something: `ando buscando piso`. In `¿Cómo andas?` it means "how are you doing?". Compared with [[caminar]]: both can mean "to walk". `caminar` is the neutral word for walking as an activity or a route. `andar` has more uses (to go around, to be doing, to work). The note flags "andar VS caminar" and gives no rule, so this comparison comes from general Spanish, not from the note.

**Example:** *Ando buscando un piso cerca del trabajo.* — I am out looking for a flat near work. *El reloj no anda.* — The clock does not work.
