**Meaning:** to wash oneself, wash

**Pattern:** Reflexive verb: requires a matching reflexive pronoun (me/te/se/nos/os/se); conjugated like *lavar* + pronoun

**Example:** *Me lavo las manos antes de comer.* — I wash my hands before eating.
