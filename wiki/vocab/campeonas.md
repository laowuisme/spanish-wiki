**Meaning:** champions (feminine plural); singular: la campeona; masculine: el campeón / los campeones

**Pattern:** Feminine plural because the group referred to is all-female. Mixed or all-male groups use `los campeones`.

**Example:** *¡Son las campeonas del torneo!* — They are the champions of the tournament!
