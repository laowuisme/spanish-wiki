**Meaning:** nowadays, these days

**Pattern:** Fixed adverbial phrase — distinct from plain [[hoy]] ("today"); refers to the current era in general, not a specific day.

**Example:** *Hoy en día casi todos usan el teléfono para pagar.* — Nowadays almost everyone uses their phone to pay.
