**Meaning:** so much, so many; as much/many

**Pattern:** Agrees in gender and number: *tanto* (masc. sg.) / *tanta* (fem. sg.) / *tantos* (masc. pl.) / *tantas* (fem. pl.). Session note had *tantas* (fem. plural). Used in comparisons: *tanto como* = as much as. *¡Tanto trabajo!* = So much work!

**Example:** *No tenía tantas revistas.* — She didn't have so many magazines.
