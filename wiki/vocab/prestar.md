**Meaning:** to lend; to loan; to pay (attention)

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. Direction is opposite to English borrow/lend: *prestar* = to lend (give temporarily), *pedir prestado* = to borrow (receive temporarily). Common expression: *prestar atención* = to pay attention.

**Example:** *¿Me puedes prestar tu bolígrafo?* — Can you lend me your pen?
