**Meaning:** scared, frightened (state)

**Pattern:** Past participle of `asustar` used as adjective. Compare with `tener miedo` (to be afraid — as a feeling). `asustado` = the state of being frightened (estar asustado); `miedo` = the feeling of fear (tener miedo).

**Example:** *Estaba muy asustada.* — She was very scared.
