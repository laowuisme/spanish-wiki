**Meaning:** skin

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `la piel`. Also used for animal "hide/fur" and "leather" in some contexts (`una chaqueta de piel`).

**Example:** *Necesito crema para la piel en invierno.* — I need cream for my skin in winter.
