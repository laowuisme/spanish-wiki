**Meaning:** funny, amusing, witty

**Pattern:** Adjective. Feminine: `graciosa`. Note: `gracioso` = funny (personality), not `divertido` (fun as in enjoyable activity).

**Example:** *Tu amigo es muy gracioso.* — Your friend is very funny.
