**Meaning:** free, off (e.g. day off)

**Pattern:** Adjective, same form for masc./fem. (ends in *-e*); plural *libres*. Common in *día libre* (day off), *tiempo libre* (free time).

**Example:** *Hoy tengo el día libre.* — Today I have the day off.
