**Meaning:** to make an effort, to try hard, to strive

**Pattern:** Stem-changing reflexive verb: o→ue in present tense: `me esfuerzo, te esfuerzas, se esfuerza, nos esforzamos, os esforzáis, se esfuerzan`. Often followed by `por + infinitive` (to strive to do something). Noun form: `el esfuerzo` (effort).

**Example:** *Se esfuerza mucho por aprender español.* — She makes a great effort to learn Spanish.
