**Meaning:** it's true

**Pattern:** Fixed expression, opposite of *es mentira*. Often used alone as agreement: *¡Es verdad!* = "That's right!"

**Example:** *Es verdad que llueve mucho aquí.* — It's true that it rains a lot here.
