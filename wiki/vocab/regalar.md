**Meaning:** to give (as a gift)

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb (regalo, regalas, regala...). Often paired with an indirect object pronoun: le regalo algo a alguien. See [[indirect-object-a-pronoun]].

**Example:** *Le regalo flores a mi madre.* — I give my mother flowers as a gift.
