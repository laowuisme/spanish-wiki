**Meaning:** stepmother

**Pattern:** Family-relation noun formed with the `-astro/-astra` suffix, which marks a step-relation (compare `padrastro` stepfather, `hermanastro/a` stepsibling — not yet in the wiki).

**Example:** *Su madrastra vive en otra ciudad.* — His stepmother lives in another city.
