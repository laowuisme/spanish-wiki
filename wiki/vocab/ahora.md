**Meaning:** now

**Pattern:** Basic time adverb. Distinctions: `ahora` (right now, at this moment), `ahora mismo` (right now, immediately — stronger emphasis), `ya` (already / now, in some contexts). Opposite: `después` (later).

**Example:** *Ahora estoy limpiando la casa.* — I'm cleaning the house right now.
