**Meaning:** relatives, family members — **false friend**: *parientes* does NOT mean "parents." "Parents" = *padres*.

**Pattern:** Regular masculine plural noun (singular *pariente*, gender-invariant for the base form, though *parienta* exists colloquially).

**Example:** *Tengo muchos parientes en México.* — I have many relatives in Mexico.
