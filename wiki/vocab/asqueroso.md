**Meaning:** disgusting, gross, revolting

**Pattern:** Adjective; feminine `asquerosa`. From the noun `asco` (disgust): `¡Qué asco!` = how gross!, `dar asco` = to disgust. A strong, informal word.

**Example:** *La comida estaba asquerosa.* — The food was disgusting.
