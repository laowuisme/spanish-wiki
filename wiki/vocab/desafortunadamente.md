**Meaning:** unfortunately

**Pattern:** Adverb formed from `desafortunado` + `-mente`; usually modifies the whole sentence and is placed at the start, followed by a comma.

**Example:** *Desafortunadamente, perdimos el vuelo.* — Unfortunately, we missed the flight.
