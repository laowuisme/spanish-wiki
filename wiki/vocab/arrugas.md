**Meaning:** wrinkles (plural); singular *la arruga* = a wrinkle

**Pattern:** Feminine noun, almost always used in plural for facial wrinkles.

**Example:** *Tiene arrugas alrededor de los ojos.* — He has wrinkles around his eyes.
