**Meaning:** later, afterwards

**Pattern:** Fixed adverbial phrase. *Más* (more) + *tarde* (late). Often used as a simple time marker. Synonyms: *luego* (then/later), *después* (after/later). *Hasta más tarde* = see you later.

**Example:** *Te llamo más tarde.* — I'll call you later.
