**Meaning:** puppy; little dog (m); affectionate diminutive of *perro*

**Pattern:** Diminutive suffix *-ito/-ita* adds smallness or affection. *perrito* (m) / *perrita* (f). *¡Qué perrito tan mono!* = What a cute little dog!

**Example:** *Ese perrito se escapa siempre que puede.* — That puppy escapes whenever it can.
