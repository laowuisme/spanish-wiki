**Meaning:** art

**Pattern:** Noun with irregular gender: masculine singular `el arte`, but feminine in the plural `las artes` (e.g. *las bellas artes*).

**Example:** *Me interesa mucho el arte moderno.* — I'm very interested in modern art.
