**Meaning:** worse, worst

**Pattern:** Comparative/superlative of `malo` (bad). Irregular: `malo → peor` (no `más malo` in standard usage). Opposite: `mejor` (better/best).

**Example:** *Es peor que ayer.* — It's worse than yesterday.
