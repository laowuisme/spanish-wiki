**Meaning:** blackboard, whiteboard (the board in a classroom)

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `la pizarra`. "On the board" is `en la pizarra`. It sits with school words such as [[regla]] (ruler).

**Example:** *La profesora escribe la fecha en la pizarra.* — The teacher writes the date on the board.
