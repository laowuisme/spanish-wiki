**Meaning:** to send

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb with spelling change: `envío, envías, envía` (i takes accent in sg/3pl to break diphthong). Synonym: `mandar`.

**Example:** *Te voy a enviar un mensaje.* — I'm going to send you a message.
