**Meaning:** many

**Pattern:** Masculine plural of `tanto` (so much/many); agrees with the noun it modifies: tanto/tanta/tantos/tantas. See [[tanto]].

**Example:** *No tenía tantos libros como ella.* — He didn't have as many books as her.
