**Meaning:** promotion (at work) (m); ascent, climb

**Pattern:** *dar un ascenso* = to give a promotion; *conseguir un ascenso* = to get a promotion. Also used for physical climbing.

**Example:** *Le dieron un ascenso porque trabajó muy duro.* — They gave her a promotion because she worked very hard.
