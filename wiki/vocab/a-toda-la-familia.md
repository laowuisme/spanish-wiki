**Meaning:** to/the whole family (as an object phrase)

**Pattern:** `toda` is the feminine singular of `todo`, agreeing with `familia`. The personal `a` appears because `familia` refers to people being affected by the verb, e.g. `invitar a toda la familia`.

**Example:** *Invitó a toda la familia a la boda.* — He invited the whole family to the wedding.
