**Meaning:** husband

**Pattern:** Family vocabulary. Synonymous with `esposo`. `marido` is slightly more informal/everyday; `esposo` is slightly more formal. Both are fully interchangeable.

**Example:** *Su marido trabaja en Madrid.* — Her husband works in Madrid.
