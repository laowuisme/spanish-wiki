**Meaning:** out of curiosity

**Pattern:** Adverbial phrase explaining motive, usually placed at the start of a question. Uses `por` to mark cause/reason, the same function `por` has in [[por-vs-para]].

**Example:** *Por curiosidad, ¿cuánto cuesta ese cuadro?* — Out of curiosity, how much does that painting cost?
