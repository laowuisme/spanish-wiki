**Meaning:** messy, untidy, disorganised

**Pattern:** Adjective; the note lists the feminine `desordenada`. The opposite is `ordenado`. `estar desordenado` is a state (a room); `ser desordenado` is a trait of a person (see [[ser-vs-estar]]).

**Example:** *Tu [[habitación]] está muy desordenada.* — Your room is very messy.
