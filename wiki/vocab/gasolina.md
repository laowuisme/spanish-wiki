**Meaning:** gasoline, petrol (f)

**Pattern:** *llenar de gasolina* = to fill up with petrol; *quedarse sin gasolina* = to run out of petrol; *gasolinera* = petrol station.

**Example:** *El coche está casi sin gasolina — necesitamos parar.* — The car is almost out of petrol — we need to stop.
