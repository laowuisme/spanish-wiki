**Meaning:** by chance, by coincidence

**Pattern:** Fixed expression: `por casualidad`. Often placed at the end of a question to soften it, e.g. `¿Sabes, por casualidad...?` (Do you happen to know...?).

**Example:** *La encontré por casualidad.* — I found it by chance.
