**Meaning:** everything

**Pattern:** Indefinite pronoun, invariable as "everything"; also functions as an adjective meaning "all/every" (`todo el día`), agreeing `todo/toda/todos/todas`.

**Example:** *Lo sé todo.* — I know everything.
