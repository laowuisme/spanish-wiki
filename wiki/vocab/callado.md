**Meaning:** quiet, silent, reserved (adj); *callado* (m) / *callada* (f)

**Pattern:** *estar callado* = to be quiet/silent (state); *quedarse callado* = to go quiet, to say nothing. *¡Cállate!* = Shut up! / Be quiet! (tú imperative of *callarse*).

**Example:** *Hoy está muy callado — ¿le pasa algo?* — He's very quiet today — is something wrong?
