**Meaning:** however, nevertheless

**Pattern:** Discourse connector. Used to introduce a contrast. Placed at the start of a clause or after a comma.

**Example:** *Me gusta el café; sin embargo, no lo tomo por la tarde.* — I like coffee; however, I don't have it in the afternoon.
