**Meaning:** at this moment, right now, currently

**Pattern:** Often used to say you can't do something right now: *en este momento no puedo* = I can't right now. Synonym: *ahora mismo*.

**Example:** *En este momento estoy en una reunión, te llamo luego.* — I'm in a meeting right now, I'll call you later.
