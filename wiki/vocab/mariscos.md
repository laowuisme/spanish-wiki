**Meaning:** seafood, shellfish

**Pattern:** Almost always used in the plural (`los mariscos`) even when referring to the food category as a whole, similar to English "seafood."

**Example:** *No come mariscos porque es alérgico.* — He doesn't eat seafood because he's allergic.
