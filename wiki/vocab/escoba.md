**Meaning:** broom

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `la escoba`. Used with `barrer` (to sweep). Related household tool: `el recogedor` (dustpan).

**Example:** *¿Dónde está la escoba? Tengo que barrer.* — Where is the broom? I need to sweep.
