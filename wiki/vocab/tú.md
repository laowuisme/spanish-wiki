**Meaning:** you

**Pattern:** Subject pronoun, informal singular "you". Distinct from [[tu]] (possessive adjective "your") — carries an accent mark to distinguish it. Often omitted since verb conjugation shows the subject.

**Example:** *Tú eres muy simpático.* — You are very nice.
