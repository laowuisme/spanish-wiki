**Meaning:** suddenly, all of a sudden

**Pattern:** Adverbial phrase. Synonyms: *de pronto*, *repentinamente* (more formal). Placed at the start or middle of a sentence. Note: in some Latin American regions *de repente* can mean "maybe" — context clarifies.

**Example:** *De repente, empezó a llover.* — Suddenly, it started to rain.
