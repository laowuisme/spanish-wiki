**Meaning:** instead of

**Pattern:** Fixed prepositional phrase followed by a noun or infinitive: *en vez de + [noun/infinitivo]*. Synonym: *en lugar de*.

**Example:** *En vez de café, tomo té.* — Instead of coffee, I drink tea.
