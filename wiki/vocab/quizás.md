**Meaning:** perhaps, maybe

**Pattern:** Synonym of *tal vez* and *a lo mejor*. When *quizás* appears before the verb, it can trigger the subjunctive (expressing uncertainty), but with indicative it simply states a possibility. Also spelled *quizá* (without the s — both are correct).

**Example:** *Quizás venga mañana.* — Maybe he'll come tomorrow. (subjunctive)

**See also:** [[tal-vez-vs-quizas]] — full comparison of both "maybe" expressions
