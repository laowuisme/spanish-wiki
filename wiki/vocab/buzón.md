**Meaning:** mailbox; letterbox; postbox

**Pattern:** Masculine noun (*el buzón*). Can refer to both a home letterbox and a street postbox. *Buzón de voz* = voicemail. *Echar una carta al buzón* = to post a letter.

**Example:** *Hay una carta en el buzón.* — There is a letter in the mailbox.
