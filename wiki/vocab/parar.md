**Meaning:** to stop; to halt; *pararse* = to come to a stop

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. *paro, paras, para*; *¡Para!* = Stop! (tú imperative). *parar de + infinitive* = to stop doing something: *para de llover* = it stops raining.

**Example:** *¡Para el coche, hay un semáforo en rojo!* — Stop the car, there's a red light!
