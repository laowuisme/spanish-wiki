**Meaning:** to hate

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. Stronger than `no gustar` (to not like). Preterite: `odié, odiaste, odió...`.

**Example:** *Odio el tráfico.* — I hate traffic. / *Los odié.* — I hated them. (raw/2026-08-08_session.md wrote "Los odie" without the accent; recorded here as the corrected preterite form.)
