**Meaning:** bachata (Dominican music and dance genre)

**Pattern:** Feminine noun (*la bachata*). Originated in the Dominican Republic. Characterised by guitar-driven rhythms and romantic themes. Related: *bailar bachata* (to dance bachata).

**Example:** *Me encanta bailar bachata.* — I love dancing bachata.
