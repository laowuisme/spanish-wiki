**Meaning:** frying pan, skillet

**Pattern:** Can be masculine or feminine depending on region: `el sartén` (Latin America) / `la sartén` (Spain). Context: kitchen/cooking vocabulary.

**Example:** *Calienta el aceite en la sartén.* — Heat the oil in the frying pan.
