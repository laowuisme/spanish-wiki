**Meaning:** uncle

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el tío`; feminine form: `la tía` (aunt). Colloquially in Spain, `tío/tía` can also mean "dude/guy".

**Example:** *Mi tío vive en Buenos Aires.* — My uncle lives in Buenos Aires.
