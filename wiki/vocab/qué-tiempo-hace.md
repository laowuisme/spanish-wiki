**Meaning:** What's the weather like

**Pattern:** Fixed weather question built with *hacer* (weather expressions use *hacer*, not *ser/estar*): *hace sol/frío/calor* = it's sunny/cold/hot.

**Example:** *¿Qué tiempo hace hoy en Madrid?* — What's the weather like today in Madrid?
