**Meaning:** to dawn, to get light; to wake up (in a place or a state); as a noun, dawn, sunrise

**Pattern:** As a verb for daybreak it is impersonal: `amanece` (it gets light). As a personal verb it means to wake up somewhere: `amanecí con fiebre`. As a noun: `el amanecer`. The note lists it twice.

**Example:** *En verano amanece muy temprano.* — In summer it gets light very early. *Vimos el amanecer desde la montaña.* — We watched the sunrise from the mountain.
