**Meaning:** by accident; without meaning to

**Pattern:** `sin` + infinitive means "without doing". The infinitive here is `querer` (to want), so the phrase says "without wanting". It describes an action you did not plan.

**Example:** *Rompí la ventana sin querer.* — I broke the window by accident.
