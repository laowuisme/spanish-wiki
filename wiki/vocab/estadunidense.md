**Meaning:** American (from the United States); a person from the US

**Pattern:** Adjective and noun. Preferred in Latin America over *americano* (which technically means anyone from the Americas). No accent on the *u*.

**Example:** *Ella es estadunidense.* — She is American (from the US).
