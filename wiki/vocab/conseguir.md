**Meaning:** to get, to obtain, to manage to (do something)

**Pattern:** Stem-changing verb (e→i, present): *consigo, consigues, consigue, conseguimos, conseguís, consiguen*. Preterite (3rd person only): *conseguí, conseguiste, **consiguió**, conseguimos, conseguisteis, **consiguieron*** — see [[preterite-irregular-verbs]]. Participle: `conseguido` — see [[present-perfect]].

**Example:** *Consiguió el trabajo.* — He/she got the job.
