**Meaning:** to carry, to wear, to take (somewhere), to have been (doing something for a time)

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb, but many meanings. See [[no-llevan-vs-tiene]] and [[confusing-llevar-llegar]].

**Example:** *Llevo una mochila.* — I'm carrying a backpack. / *Llevo dos años aquí.* — I've been here for two years.
