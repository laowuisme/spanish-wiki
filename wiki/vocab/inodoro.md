**Meaning:** toilet (the fixture)

**Pattern:** Masculine noun. Formal/technical term. Common alternatives: `el baño` (bathroom, the room), `el servicio` (restroom), `el WC`. In everyday speech, `el baño` is the safe default.

**Example:** *El inodoro está al fondo del pasillo.* — The toilet is at the end of the hallway.
