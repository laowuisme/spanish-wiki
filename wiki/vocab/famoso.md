**Meaning:** celebrated, famous, celebrity

**Pattern:** Adjective (famoso/famosa/famosos/famosas); also used as a noun meaning "celebrity" (el famoso / la famosa).

**Example:** *Ese actor es muy famoso en todo el mundo.* — That actor is very famous all over the world.
