**Meaning:** curly

**Pattern:** Adjective; feminine `rizada`. Most often with hair: `pelo rizado`. The opposite is `liso` (straight). The verb is `rizar` (to curl). See [[pelo]].

**Example:** *Mi hermana tiene el pelo rizado.* — My sister has curly hair.
