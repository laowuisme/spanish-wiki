**Meaning:** sugar

**Pattern:** Usually masculine (`el azúcar`), though it's one of the few nouns commonly heard as feminine in some regions (`la azúcar`).

**Example:** *¿Me pasas el azúcar, por favor?* — Can you pass me the sugar, please?
