**Meaning:** secret

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el secreto`; plural `los secretos`. Also an adjective meaning 'secret, hidden': `secreto/secreta`.

**Example:** *Te voy a contar un secreto.* — I'm going to tell you a secret.
