**Meaning:** to jump, to leap, to skip (over)

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. *salto, saltas, salta, saltamos, saltan.* Also used figuratively: *saltar un turno* (to skip a turn), *saltar a la vista* (to be obvious, lit. to jump to the eye).

**Example:** *El niño salta muy alto.* — The child jumps very high.
