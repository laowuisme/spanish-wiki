**Meaning:** many times, often, frequently

**Pattern:** *Muchas* (many, fem. pl.) + *veces* (times). Frequency adverb phrase. Similar alternatives: *a menudo* (often), *frecuentemente* (frequently), *con frecuencia* (frequently). *Muchas veces* is the most colloquial.

**Example:** *Muchas veces como aquí.* — I eat here often.
