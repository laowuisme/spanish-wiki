**Meaning:** what a relief!

**Pattern:** [[qué-exclamations]] pattern: *¡Qué + noun!* → *¡Qué alivio!* Note: *alivio* = relief (m); *con alivio* = with relief.

**Example:** *¡Qué alivio! Ya paró de llover.* — What a relief! It stopped raining.
