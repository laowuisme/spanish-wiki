**Meaning:** selfish, self-centred; egotist (n)

**Pattern:** Same form for m/f: *es muy egoísta* (he/she is very selfish). Can also be used as a noun: *es un egoísta* = he's a selfish person.

**Example:** *Es muy egoísta — solo piensa en sí mismo.* — He's very selfish — he only thinks about himself.
