**Meaning:** to jog, to trot

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. `trotar` is slightly more informal than `correr` (to run) for light jogging. Related: [[acampar]], [[al-aire-libre]].

**Example:** *Troto media hora cada mañana.* — I jog half an hour every morning.
