**Meaning:** rude, crude, coarse

**Pattern:** Adjective. Similar to `maleducado` but `grosero` implies more deliberate rudeness or vulgarity. Feminine: `grosera`.

**Example:** *Fue muy grosero con el camarero.* — He was very rude to the waiter.
