**Meaning:** balcony

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el balcón`. Common in apartment/home vocabulary. Related: `la terraza` (larger terrace/patio).

**Example:** *Salgo al balcón a tomar el aire.* — I go out to the balcony to get some fresh air.
