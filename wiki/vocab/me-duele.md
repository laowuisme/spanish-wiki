**Meaning:** it hurts me / I have pain in... (my [body part] hurts)

**Pattern:** `doler` works like `gustar` — the body part is the subject. `Me duele + singular body part` / `Me duelen + plural body parts`. See [[gusta-followed-by-clause]].

**Example:** *Me duele la cabeza.* — My head hurts. / *Me duelen los pies.* — My feet hurt.
