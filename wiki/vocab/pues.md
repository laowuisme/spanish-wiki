**Meaning:** well, so, then; indicates consequence, hesitation, or continuation

**Pattern:** Very common filler and connector. Three main uses:
- **Consequence**: *Pues, vamos.* = Well, let's go then.
- **Hesitation/thinking aloud**: *Pues… no sé.* = Well… I don't know.
- **Because/since** (formal, literary): *Lo hago pues es necesario.* = I do it because it's necessary.

**Example:** *¿No tienes dinero? Pues no puedes comprar nada.* — You have no money? Well, then you can't buy anything.
