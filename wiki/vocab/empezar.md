**Meaning:** to start, begin; *empieza a + infinitive* = starts to / begins to

**Pattern:** Stem-changing (e→ie): *empiezo, empiezas, empieza, empezamos, empiezan*. *empieza a llover* = it starts raining; *empiezo a entender* = I'm starting to understand.

**Example:** *Empieza a llover — busca el impermeable.* — It's starting to rain — find the raincoat.
