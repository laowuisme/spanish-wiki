**Meaning:** glass (the material, not a drinking glass)

**Pattern:** Used with `de vidrio` to describe what an object is made of — a material noun, not a countable object.

**Example:** *La botella se rompió porque es de vidrio.* — The bottle broke because it's made of glass.
