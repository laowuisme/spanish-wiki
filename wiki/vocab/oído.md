**Meaning:** (inner) ear; hearing

**Pattern:** Body/health vocabulary. `oído` = inner ear / hearing faculty; `oreja` = outer ear (what you see). `me duele el oído` = I have earache.

**Example:** *Me duele el oído izquierdo.* — My left ear hurts.
