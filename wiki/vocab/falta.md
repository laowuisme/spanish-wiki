**Meaning:** is lacking / is missing (3rd-person singular present of *faltar*); also used impersonally in *hace falta* = "it's necessary"

**Pattern:** [[faltar-construction]] — *faltar* behaves like *gustar*: the person experiencing the lack takes an indirect object pronoun, and the thing/person missing is the grammatical subject the verb agrees with.

**Example:** *Faltan Diego y Ana.* — Diego and Ana are missing. (plural *faltan* because the subject is plural)
