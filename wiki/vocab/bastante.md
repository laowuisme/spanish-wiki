**Meaning:** quite, rather, enough, fairly

**Pattern:** Can modify adjectives (`bastante difícil` = quite difficult) or verbs. As adjective: `bastante(s)` = enough (invariable for gender, adds -s for plural).

**Example:** *Habla bastante bien.* — He speaks quite well.
