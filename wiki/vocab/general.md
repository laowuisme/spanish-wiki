**Meaning:** general (military rank); general/overall (adjective); *en general* = in general, generally

**Pattern:** As noun: *el general* (the general). As adjective: invariable in meaning but agrees in gender: *una visión general* = a general overview. Key phrase: *en general* = in general, usually placed at the start or end of a sentence.

**Example:** *En general, me gusta la comida española.* — In general, I like Spanish food.
