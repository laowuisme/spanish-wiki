**Meaning:** to iron (clothes)

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. Noun: `la plancha` (iron, the appliance). Common household chore alongside `lavar`, `secar`, and `colgar la ropa`.

**Example:** *Tengo que planchar la camisa antes de la reunión.* — I need to iron the shirt before the meeting.
