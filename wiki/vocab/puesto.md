**Meaning:** position, post, job; market stall; past participle of `poner`

**Pattern:** Multiple uses: (1) `un puesto de trabajo` = a job position; (2) `tener puesto` = to be wearing (see [[tener-expressions]]); (3) `puesto` = market stall. Flagged as hard to remember.

**Example:** *Tiene puesto un sombrero.* — He/she is wearing a hat. / *Busco un nuevo puesto.* — I'm looking for a new position.
