**Meaning:** never; ever (emphatic)

**Pattern:** Stronger than [[nunca]]. It follows the same placement rule as `nadie` and `nada` (see [[nadie-nada-negation]]): before the verb there is no `no` (`Jamás lo olvidaré`), after the verb `no` is needed (`No lo olvidaré jamás`). The set phrase `para siempre jamás` means forever and ever. The note lists it twice.

**Example:** *Jamás he visto un lugar tan bonito.* — I have never seen such a beautiful place.
