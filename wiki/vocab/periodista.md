**Meaning:** journalist; reporter
**Pattern:** *-ista* nouns are masculine/feminine by article — *el/la periodista*; plural: *los periodistas* (masculine or mixed group)
**Example:** *Los periodistas cubrieron la noticia.* — The journalists covered the news.
