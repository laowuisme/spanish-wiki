**Meaning:** all the time, always, constantly

**Pattern:** Fixed phrase. *Todo* (all) + *el tiempo* (the time). Compare with *siempre* (always) — interchangeable in many contexts but *todo el tiempo* emphasises continuity.

**Example:** *Está lloviendo todo el tiempo.* — It's raining all the time.
