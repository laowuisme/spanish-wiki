**Meaning:** (it) sounds like, looks like, seems like

**Pattern:** Fixed expression from `parecer` (to seem) + `que`; introduces a clause: `parece que + [clause]`.

**Example:** *Parece que va a llover.* — It looks like it's going to rain.
