**Meaning:** your

**Pattern:** Possessive adjective (informal singular); invariable for gender, takes -s for plural: `tu/tus`. Distinct from [[tú]] (subject pronoun "you") — no accent mark.

**Example:** *¿Dónde está tu casa?* — Where is your house?
