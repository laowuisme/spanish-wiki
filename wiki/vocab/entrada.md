**Meaning:** ticket

**Pattern:** Feminine noun (*la entrada*), plural *entradas*. Also means "entrance" or, on a menu, "starter/appetizer".

**Example:** *Compré dos entradas para el concierto.* — I bought two tickets for the concert.
