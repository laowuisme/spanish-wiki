**Meaning:** wet, soaked, drenched

**Pattern:** Gender agreement: *mojado* (m) / *mojada* (f). *estar mojado* = to be wet (state).

**Example:** *Estoy mojada porque no llevaba impermeable.* — I'm wet because I wasn't wearing a raincoat.
