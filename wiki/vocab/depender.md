**Meaning:** to depend (on)

**Pattern:** Regular -er verb. `depender de` = to depend on. `depende` used alone as a standalone response = "it depends." `depende del tiempo` = it depends on the weather.

**Example:** *¿Vas a la playa mañana? Depende.* — Are you going to the beach tomorrow? It depends.
