**Meaning:** early morning hours (roughly 2am–6am); small hours of the night

**Pattern:** Feminine noun. `de madrugada` = in the early hours / at dawn. Distinct from `mañana` (morning, 6am–12pm) and `amanecer` (sunrise/daybreak).

**Example:** *Llegó a casa de madrugada.* — He arrived home in the early hours of the morning.
