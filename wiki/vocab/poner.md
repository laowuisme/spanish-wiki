**Meaning:** to put, to place, to set; also: to put on (clothes — ponerse)

**Pattern:** Irregular verb. First person singular: `pongo`. Past participle: `puesto`. `ponerse` = to put on / to become (emotionally).

**Example:** *Pon el libro en la mesa.* — Put the book on the table. / *Me pongo el abrigo.* — I put on my coat.
