**Meaning:** thief, robber

**Pattern:** Masculine noun; feminine `ladrona`. The plural `ladrones` loses the written accent (a word ending in -n keeps its stress on the same syllable without the mark).

**Example:** *El ladrón entró por la ventana.* — The thief came in through the window.
