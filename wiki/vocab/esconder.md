**Meaning:** to hide (something); reflexive *esconderse* = to hide oneself

**Pattern:** Regular -er verb. *Esconder algo* = to hide something (transitive). *Esconderse* = to hide oneself. See [[reflexive-verbs]] for the reflexive pattern.

**Example:** *¿Dónde escondiste las llaves?* — Where did you hide the keys?
