**Meaning:** briefcase; small case

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el maletín`. It is the small form of [[maleta]] (suitcase). The plural drops the accent: `maletines`.

**Example:** *El maletín del abogado estaba lleno de papeles.* — The lawyer's briefcase was full of papers.
