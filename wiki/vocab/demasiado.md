**Meaning:** too much, too many; too (adv)

**Pattern:** As adverb (invariable): *comes demasiado* = you eat too much; *Te quejas demasiado* = you complain too much. As adjective (agrees with noun): *demasiada gente* = too many people; *demasiados problemas* = too many problems.

**Example:** *Te quejas demasiado — intenta ser más positivo.* — You complain too much — try to be more positive.
