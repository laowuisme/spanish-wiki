**Meaning:** younger; also "minor" (someone under legal age). Comparative of *joven* (young) — Spanish uses *menor* directly instead of *más joven*.

**Pattern:** Invariable adjective ending in -or (same form for masculine/feminine): *el/la menor*. Commonly paired with the [[se-ve-construction]]: *Se ve menor* — "S/he looks younger."

**Example:** *Se ve menor. No sé su edad.* — S/he looks younger. I don't know their age.
