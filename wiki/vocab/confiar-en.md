**Meaning:** trust, trusted

**Pattern:** Verb + preposition: `confiar en` (confiar takes a written accent in most present-tense forms: `confío, confías, confía...`). Affirmative tú command: `confía en mí` = "trust me."

**Example:** *Confío en ti.* — I trust you. *Confía en mí, todo va a salir bien.* — Trust me, everything's going to be fine.
