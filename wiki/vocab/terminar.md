**Meaning:** to finish, to end

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. `termina` = he/she/it finishes (3rd sg present). `terminar de + infinitive` = to finish doing something: *termina de comer* (finish eating), *¿terminaste de leer?* (did you finish reading?).

**Example:** *La clase termina a las dos.* — Class finishes at two.
