**Meaning:** to propose, to suggest

**Pattern:** Irregular verb conjugated like `poner` (`propongo, propones...`; preterite `propuse`).

**Example:** *Le voy a proponer un plan nuevo a mi jefe.* — I'm going to propose a new plan to my boss.
