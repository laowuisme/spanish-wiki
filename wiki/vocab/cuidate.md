**Meaning:** take care (of yourself) — informal farewell

**Pattern:** Imperative of the reflexive verb *cuidarse*. See [[reflexive-verbs]] for how reflexive imperatives work. Formal equivalent: *cuídese*.

**Example:** *¡Cuídate mucho!* — Take good care of yourself!
