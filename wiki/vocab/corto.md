**Meaning:** short (length, duration) — NOT short in stature (that is `bajo`)

**Pattern:** Adjective. Opposite of `largo` (long). Feminine: `corta`. The word keeps coming back as `corta` — see [[forgets-corto]].

**Example:** *El pelo largo VS corto.* — Long VS short hair.
