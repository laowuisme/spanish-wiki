**Meaning:** noise, sound (m)

**Pattern:** *hacer ruido* = to make noise; *hay mucho ruido* = it's very noisy; *un ruido extraño* = a strange noise. Adjective: *ruidoso/a* = noisy.

**Example:** *Hay mucho ruido en esta calle, no puedo dormir.* — There's a lot of noise on this street, I can't sleep.
