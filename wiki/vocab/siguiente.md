**Meaning:** next, following; invariable for gender (same form for masculine and feminine)

**Pattern:** `el/la siguiente` (the next one), `la siguiente semana` (the following week). Contrast: `próximo/a` (upcoming/next in time) vs `siguiente` (the one that follows in sequence).

**Example:** *El siguiente partido es el jueves.* — The next match is on Thursday.
