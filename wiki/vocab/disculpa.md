**Meaning:** excuse me

**Pattern:** Informal (tú) command form of `disculpar`, used to apologize or get someone's attention. Also a noun, `la disculpa` ("apology"). Compare [[disculpe-vs-disculpen]] for the formal forms.

**Example:** *Disculpa, ¿tienes hora?* — Excuse me, do you have the time?
