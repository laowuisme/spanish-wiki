**Meaning:** Russian

**Pattern:** Adjective of nationality; agrees in gender/number: `ruso/rusa/rusos/rusas`. `El ruso` (masculine noun) also names the language.

**Example:** *Mi vecino es ruso.* — My neighbor is Russian.
