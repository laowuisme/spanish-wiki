**Meaning:** sponge (for cleaning)

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `la esponja`. Plural: `las esponjas`. Common in kitchen/cleaning contexts.

**Example:** *Limpia la sartén con una esponja.* — Clean the frying pan with a sponge.
