**Meaning:** shopping list

**Pattern:** *La lista* (the list) + *de la compra* (of the shopping). *La compra* = the shopping/groceries. *Hacer la compra* = to do the shopping. *Hacer una lista de la compra* = to make a shopping list.

**Example:** *No olvides la lista de la compra.* — Don't forget the shopping list.
