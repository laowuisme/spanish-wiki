**Meaning:** happy, cheerful

**Pattern:** Invariable adjective ending in -e (same form for masculine/feminine): *alegre*, plural *alegres*. Use with **estar** for a temporary mood (*está alegre*) or with **ser** for a naturally cheerful personality (*es alegre*) — see [[ser-vs-estar]].

**Example:** *Está muy alegre hoy.* — She's very cheerful today.
