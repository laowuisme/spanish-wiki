**Meaning:** peaches

**Pattern:** Plural of masculine noun `el durazno`; regional term used mainly in Latin America (Spain typically uses `melocotón`).

**Example:** *Compré duraznos frescos en el mercado.* — I bought fresh peaches at the market.
