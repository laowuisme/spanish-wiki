**Meaning:** anyway, in short, well...; used to summarise, wrap up a thought, or shift topic — similar to "in any case" or "anyway" in English

**Pattern:** Discourse marker, usually at the start of a sentence or clause. Signals the speaker is concluding or moving on.

**Example:** *En fin, mañana veremos qué pasa.* — Anyway, tomorrow we'll see what happens.
