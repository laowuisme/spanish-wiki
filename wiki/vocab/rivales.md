**Meaning:** rivals, opponents; singular: el/la rival (same form for both genders)

**Pattern:** `los rivales` (masculine/mixed) or `las rivales` (all-female). `rival` is invariable for gender — only the article changes.

**Example:** *Nuestros rivales son muy buenos este año.* — Our rivals are very good this year.
