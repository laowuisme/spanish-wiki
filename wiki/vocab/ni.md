**Meaning:** nor; neither … nor; not even

**Pattern:** `ni` joins two negative ideas. After the verb, `no` must come before the verb: `No como carne ni pescado.` With `ni … ni …` before the verb, `no` is dropped: `Ni mi hermano ni yo hablamos francés.` This is the same placement rule as [[nunca]] (see [[nadie-nada-negation]]). `ni siquiera` means "not even". The fixed phrase `ni idea` has its own page: [[ni-idea]]. The note lists only the bare word `ni`, with no sentence.

**Example:** *No como carne ni pescado.* — I eat neither meat nor fish.
