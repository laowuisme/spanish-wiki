**Meaning:** river

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el río`. The written accent marks a hiatus (rí-o, two syllables) rather than the diphthong that 'rio' would otherwise form.

**Example:** *El río Amazonas es muy largo.* — The Amazon River is very long.
