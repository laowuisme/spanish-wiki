**Meaning:** healthy

**Pattern:** Can pair with either `ser` (health as an inherent trait: *Es una persona sana*) or `estar` (current state of health: *Está sano ahora*) — see [[ser-vs-estar]].

**Example:** *Como frutas y verduras para estar sano.* — I eat fruits and vegetables to be healthy.
