**Meaning:** the same; equal

**Pattern:** The form is the same for masculine and feminine. The plural is `iguales`. `igual que` means "the same as" or "just like". In `me da igual` the word means "I do not mind" — see [[dar-expressions]].

**Example:** *Las dos camisas son iguales.* — The two shirts are the same.
