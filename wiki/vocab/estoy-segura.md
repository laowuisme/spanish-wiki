**Meaning:** I'm sure / I'm certain (said by a female speaker); masculine form: estoy seguro

**Pattern:** `estar + seguro/a` — uses [[estar]] because it describes a current mental state. `segura` (feminine) vs `seguro` (masculine) — the ending agrees with the speaker's gender.

**Example:** *Estoy segura de que va a ganar.* — I'm sure she's going to win.
