**Meaning:** walnut, nut (la nuez); irregular plural nueces

**Pattern:** Feminine noun with -z → -ces plural spelling change, same pattern as luz → luces.

**Example:** *Compré un montón de nueces.* — I bought a bunch of nuts. See [[montón-de]].
