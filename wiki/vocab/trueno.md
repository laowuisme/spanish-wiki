**Meaning:** thunder (m); thunderclap

**Pattern:** *los truenos* = thunder (plural); *hay truenos* = it's thundering. Paired with [[tormenta]] and [[relámpago]] (lightning).

**Example:** *Hay mucho trueno esta noche.* — There's a lot of thunder tonight.
