**Meaning:** I watered (preterite of regar — to water plants)

**Pattern:** Verb `regar` (to water) — stem changes to e→ie in present, and spelling change g→gu before e in preterite to maintain sound: `regué` (not `regé`).

**Example:** *Regué las plantas esta mañana.* — I watered the plants this morning.
