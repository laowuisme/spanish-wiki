**Meaning:** toward, in the direction of

**Pattern:** Indicates motion in a direction without necessarily implying arrival — contrast `a`, which implies reaching the destination itself. See [[por-vs-para]] for other direction/purpose prepositions.

**Example:** *Conduce hacia el parque y luego gira a la izquierda.* — Drive toward the park and then turn left.
