**Meaning:** to add

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb with -gar spelling change in preterite yo-form (agregué), same pattern as [[regar]]. See [[conjugation-spelling-patterns]]. `agregó` = preterite él/ella form ("he/she added"), seen in raw notes.

**Example:** *Agregó sal a la sopa.* — He/she added salt to the soup.
