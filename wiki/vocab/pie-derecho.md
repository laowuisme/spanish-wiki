**Meaning:** right foot

**Pattern:** *El pie* ([[pie]] — foot) + *derecho* (right, as in direction; also means "straight" or "right/correct"). Note: *derecho* here is an adjective agreeing with *pie* (masculine). The noun *la derecha* = the right side. See also [[derecha]].

**Example:** *Me duele el pie derecho.* — My right foot hurts.
