**Meaning:** floor (of a building); flat/apartment (Spain); also: floor/ground surface

**Pattern:** Housing vocabulary. `primer piso` = first floor. In Spain, `piso` = apartment.

**Example:** *Vivo en el tercer piso.* — I live on the third floor.
