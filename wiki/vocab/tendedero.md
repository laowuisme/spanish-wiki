**Meaning:** drying rack, clotheshorse / clothesline (outdoor)

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el tendedero`. Indoor version = drying rack; outdoor version = clothesline. Related verb: `tender la ropa` (to hang out the laundry).

**Example:** *Cuelga la ropa en el tendedero para que se seque.* — Hang the clothes on the rack so they dry.
