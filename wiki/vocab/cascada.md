**Meaning:** waterfall

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `una cascada`. A very large waterfall is also called `catarata`.

**Example:** *Caminamos dos horas hasta llegar a la cascada.* — We walked two hours to reach the waterfall. See [[caminar]].
