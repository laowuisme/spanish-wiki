**Meaning:** microwave (the appliance)

**Pattern:** Invariable noun — same form in singular and plural (`el microondas` / `los microondas`), since it's built from `micro` + `ondas` (waves).

**Example:** *Calenté la sopa en el microondas.* — I heated the soup in the microwave.
