**Meaning:** hears, hey

**Pattern:** Informal interjection used to get someone's attention (like "hey" or "listen"); also the 3rd-person singular present of `oír` (to hear).

**Example:** *¡Oye! ¿Adónde vas?* — Hey! Where are you going?
