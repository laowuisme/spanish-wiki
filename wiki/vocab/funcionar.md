**Meaning:** to work, to function, to operate; *funciona* = it works

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. *funciona bien* = it works well; *no funciona* = it doesn't work; *¿Funciona?* = Does it work?

**Example:** *El ascensor no funciona — tenemos que usar las escaleras.* — The lift isn't working — we have to use the stairs.
