**Meaning:** any, whichever

**Pattern:** Apocopated form of *cualquiera*, used before singular nouns (masculine or feminine): *cualquier libro*, *cualquier persona*. Keeps the full form *cualquiera* when it doesn't precede a noun. A relative/free-choice indefinite, distinct from the affirmative/negative pairs in [[algo-series]].

**Example:** *Puedes elegir cualquier libro.* — You can choose any book.
