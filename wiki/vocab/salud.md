**Meaning:** cheers

**Pattern:** Feminine noun meaning 'health' (`la salud`); also used as an interjection `¡Salud!` ('Cheers!'/'Bless you!') when toasting or after a sneeze.

**Example:** *¡Salud! Por los nuevos amigos.* — Cheers! To new friends.
