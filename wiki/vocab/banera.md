**Meaning:** bathtub

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `la bañera`. Bathroom vocabulary. Related: `ducharse` (to shower), `darse un baño` (to take a bath). In some regions also called `la tina`.

**Example:** *¿Prefieres ducharte o darte un baño en la bañera?* — Do you prefer to shower or take a bath in the tub?
