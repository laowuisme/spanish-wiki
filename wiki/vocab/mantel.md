**Meaning:** tablecloth

**Pattern:** Masculine noun (`el mantel`) despite the -el ending; used with `poner el mantel` (to lay the tablecloth) when setting a table.

**Example:** *Puso un mantel nuevo antes de la cena.* — She put out a new tablecloth before dinner.
