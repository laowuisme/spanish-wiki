**Meaning:** to dry

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. Reflexive form: `secarse` = to dry oneself/dry up. Common in laundry and personal hygiene contexts. Adjective form: `seco/a` (dry).

**Example:** *Cuelga la ropa para que se seque.* — Hang the clothes so they dry.
