**Meaning:** to send; to order, to command

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. *mando, mandas, manda.* Two main uses: (1) to send something/someone (*mandar un correo* = to send an email); (2) to be in charge / give orders (*aquí mando yo* = I'm in charge here). Imperative + clitic: *mándame* = send me.

**Example:** *Mándame un correo electrónico.* — Send me an email.
