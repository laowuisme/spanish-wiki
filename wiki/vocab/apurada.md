**Meaning:** hurried, rushed; (in some Latin American dialects) in trouble

**Pattern:** Past participle of `apurarse` (to hurry) used as adjective; agrees in gender/number (`apurado/apurada/apurados/apuradas`). Used with `estar` for the temporary state, not `ser` — see [[ser-vs-estar]].

**Example:** *Estoy apurada, no tengo tiempo.* — I'm in a hurry, I don't have time.
