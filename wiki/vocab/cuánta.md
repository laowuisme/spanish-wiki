**Meaning:** how much

**Pattern:** Interrogative adjective/pronoun, feminine singular; agrees with the noun it modifies: cuánto/cuánta/cuántos/cuántas.

**Example:** *¿Cuánta agua necesitas?* — How much water do you need?
