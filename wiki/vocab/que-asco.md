**Meaning:** how disgusting! / that's gross! — exclamation of disgust

**Pattern:** ¡Qué + noun! exclamation pattern — see [[qué-exclamations]].

**Example:** *¡Qué asco, hay una cucaracha!* — How disgusting, there's a cockroach!
