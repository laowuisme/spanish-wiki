**Meaning:** pen (Mexico/Latin America); also feather

**Pattern:** Noun, feminine: *la pluma*. **Regional:** *pluma* = pen in Mexico and much of Latin America. In Spain and formal contexts, use *[[bolígrafo]]* for ballpoint pen. *pluma* literally means feather, hence its use for writing instruments.

**Clarification:** *pluma* vs *bolígrafo* — both mean pen, but pluma is the Latin American term; bolígrafo is the Spanish/formal standard.

**Example:** *¿Tienes una pluma? Necesito firmar esto.* — Do you have a pen? I need to sign this.
