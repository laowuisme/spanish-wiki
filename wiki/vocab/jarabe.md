**Meaning:** syrup (medicine); cough syrup

**Pattern:** Noun, masculine: *el jarabe*. Most commonly heard as *jarabe para la tos* = cough syrup. Also used for sweet syrups in cooking.

**Example:** *Toma este jarabe tres veces al día.* — Take this syrup three times a day.
