**Meaning:** sunscreen

**Pattern:** Masculine noun. `crema solar` has the same meaning. Typical verbs: `ponerse protector solar`, `echarse protector solar`. `solar` = of the sun (see [[sol]]).

**Example:** *No olvides ponerte protector solar antes de salir.* — Do not forget to put on sunscreen before going out.
