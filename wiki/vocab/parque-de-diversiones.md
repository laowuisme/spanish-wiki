**Meaning:** amusement park

**Pattern:** Fixed compound noun built on [[parque]] ("park") + `de diversiones` ("of amusements").

**Example:** *Fuimos al parque de diversiones y subimos a la montaña rusa.* — We went to the amusement park and rode the roller coaster.
