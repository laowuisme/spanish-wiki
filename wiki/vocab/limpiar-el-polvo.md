**Meaning:** to dust (literally "to clean the dust")

**Pattern:** [[limpiar]] + `el polvo` ([[polvo]]). Spanish has no single verb for "to dust". Add the thing you dust with `de`: `limpiar el polvo de los muebles`. `quitar el polvo` means the same.

**Example:** *Los sábados limpio el polvo de los muebles.* — On Saturdays I dust the furniture.
