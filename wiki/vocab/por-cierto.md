**Meaning:** by the way

**Pattern:** Discourse marker introducing a related but tangential comment. Not to be confused with `cierto` used adjectivally (certain/true).

**Example:** *Por cierto, ¿viste el letrero nuevo en la tienda?* — By the way, did you see the new sign at the store?
