**Meaning:** tent (camping, Latin America); large event tent; carp (fish)

**Pattern:** Feminine noun. In Spain a camping tent is `tienda de campaña`. A `carpa` is also the big tent used at events and circuses.

**Example:** *Montamos la carpa junto al río.* — We pitched the tent next to the river.
