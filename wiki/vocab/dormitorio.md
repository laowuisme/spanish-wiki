**Meaning:** bedroom

**Pattern:** Rooms vocabulary. Synonymous with `habitación` and `cuarto` in most contexts. `Dormitorio` is slightly more formal. See also [[habitación]], [[cuarto]].

**Example:** *El dormitorio tiene dos ventanas.* — The bedroom has two windows.
