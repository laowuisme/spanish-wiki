**Meaning:** flat tire (a tire with a puncture)

**Pattern:** Noun + participle used as an adjective. `pinchado` comes from `pinchar` (to puncture) and agrees with the noun: `un neumático pinchado`, `una rueda pinchada`. In the present perfect the same participle does not change — see [[present-perfect]]. `neumático` is the standard word for the tire. In many Latin American countries, `llanta` is the usual word.

**Example:** *El coche tiene un neumático pinchado.* — The car has a flat tire. See [[coche]].
