**Meaning:** anyway, in any case, regardless

**Pattern:** Discourse connector — used to dismiss a previous point and assert what follows. Related to [[por-vs-para]] (connector adverbs).

**Example:** *De todas formas, voy a ir.* — Anyway, I'm going to go.
