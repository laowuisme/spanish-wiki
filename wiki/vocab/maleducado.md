**Meaning:** rude, ill-mannered, badly raised

**Pattern:** Adjective. Feminine: `maleducada`. Formed from `mal` (bad) + `educado` (educated/raised).

**Example:** *Es muy maleducado no dar las gracias.* — It's very rude not to say thank you.
