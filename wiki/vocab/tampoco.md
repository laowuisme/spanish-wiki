**Meaning:** neither, not either, either (in negative context)

**Pattern:** Negative counterpart to `también` (also). Used in negative sentences: where `también` = "also", `tampoco` = "neither/not either."

**Example:** *Yo tampoco quiero ir.* — I don't want to go either. / *A mí tampoco me gusta.* — I don't like it either.
