**Meaning:** barbecue, roast

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el asado` (especially common in Argentina/Uruguay for a barbecue gathering). Also exists as the past participle/adjective `asado/a` meaning "roasted, grilled".

**Example:** *Vamos a hacer un asado el sábado.* — We're going to have a barbecue on Saturday.
