**Meaning:** tree

**Pattern:** Masculine noun (*el árbol*). Plural: *los árboles*. Note the accent on the á — required because the stress falls on the first syllable (*ÁR-bol*).

**Example:** *Hay un árbol grande en el jardín.* — There is a big tree in the garden.
