**Meaning:** you feel like (doing something); you want to; you're in the mood for
**Pattern:** *tener ganas de* + infinitive — *tengo/tienes/tiene ganas de* + infinitive. See [[tener-expressions]]. The phrase came back as `tengo ganas` on 2026-10-29 — see [[forgets-tienes-ganas]].
**Example:** *¿Tienes ganas de salir esta noche?* — Do you feel like going out tonight?
**Example:** *Tengo ganas de escalar una montaña.* — I feel like climbing a mountain.
