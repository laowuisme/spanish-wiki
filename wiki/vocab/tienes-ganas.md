**Meaning:** you feel like (doing something); you want to; you're in the mood for
**Pattern:** *tener ganas de* + infinitive — *tengo/tienes/tiene ganas de* + infinitive
**Example:** *¿Tienes ganas de salir esta noche?* — Do you feel like going out tonight?
