**Meaning:** to start (an engine, a car, a machine); to pull out, to tear off

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb with a spelling change: preterite yo `arranqué` (c becomes qu before e, see [[conjugation-spelling-patterns]]). The note lists the form `arranca` (it starts) and gives no sentence.

**Example:** *El coche no arranca por la mañana.* — The car does not start in the morning.
