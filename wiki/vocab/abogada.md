**Meaning:** (female) lawyer, attorney

**Pattern:** Gendered profession noun. *El abogado* (male lawyer) / *la abogada* (female lawyer). Regular -o/-a gender switch. See [[traitor-nouns]] for exceptions to this pattern (this word is regular).

**Example:** *Mi hermana es abogada.* — My sister is a lawyer.
