**Meaning:** sky; heaven; darling (term of endearment)

**Pattern:** Masculine noun (*el cielo*). Three uses: (1) the sky (*el cielo está despejado* = the sky is clear); (2) heaven (*ir al cielo* = to go to heaven); (3) affectionate address (*¡cielo!* = darling/sweetheart, similar to *cariño*).

**Example:** *El cielo está lleno de estrellas.* — The sky is full of stars.
