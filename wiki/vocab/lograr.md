**Meaning:** to manage to, to succeed in, to achieve

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. Often followed directly by an infinitive: `lograr + infinitivo` = "to manage to [verb]."

**Example:** *Al final logré terminar el proyecto a tiempo.* — In the end I managed to finish the project on time.
