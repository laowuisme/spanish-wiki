**Meaning:** similar, alike

**Pattern:** Adjective from [[parecer]]. `parecido a` = similar to. The verb `parecerse a` means to look like. `Son muy parecidos` = they are very alike.

**Example:** *Mi hermano es muy parecido a mi padre.* — My brother looks a lot like my father.
