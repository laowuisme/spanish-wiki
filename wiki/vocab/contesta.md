**Meaning:** answer!, reply! — tú imperative of *contestar* (to answer)

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. *Contesta* = informal command. *Conteste* = formal (usted). Also used as 3rd-person present: *ella contesta* (she answers). Compare with *responder* (to respond/reply) — largely interchangeable.

**Example:** *¡Contesta el teléfono!* — Answer the phone!
