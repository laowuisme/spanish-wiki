**Meaning:** certain, true; sure; a certain (before noun)

**Pattern:** Multiple uses: (1) *Es cierto.* = It's true/certain. (2) *cierto + noun* = a certain (e.g., *cierta persona* = a certain person). (3) *por cierto* = by the way.

**Example:** *Es cierto que habla español muy bien.* — It's true that she speaks Spanish very well.
