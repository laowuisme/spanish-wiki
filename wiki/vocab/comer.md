**Meaning:** to eat

**Pattern:** Regular `-er` verb: `como, comes, come...` In the affirmative tú command, object pronouns attach to the end: `cómetelos` = "eat them up" (masc plural object). See [[affirmative-commands]].

**Example:** *Vamos a comer a las dos.* — We're going to eat at two. *Estos tacos están deliciosos — cómetelos antes de que se enfríen.* — These tacos are delicious — eat them before they get cold.
