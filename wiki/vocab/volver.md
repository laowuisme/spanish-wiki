**Meaning:** to return, to come back; `volver a + infinitive` = to do something again

**Pattern:** Stem-changing verb (o→ue): `vuelvo, vuelves, vuelve, volvemos, volvéis, vuelven`. `vuelves` (2nd sg), `vuelve` (3rd sg). See [[llega-vs-vuelve]].

**Example:** *¿Cuándo vuelves?* — When are you coming back? / *Vuelve a llamar.* — He calls again.
