**Meaning:** how much

**Pattern:** Interrogative adjective/pronoun, masculine singular; also used as an adverb (¿cuánto cuesta?). Agrees: cuánto/cuánta/cuántos/cuántas.

**Example:** *¿Cuánto tiempo tienes?* — How much time do you have?
