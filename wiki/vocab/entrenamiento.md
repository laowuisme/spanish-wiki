**Meaning:** training, workout; practice session

**Pattern:** Masculine noun (*el entrenamiento*). From the verb *entrenar(se)* (to train). *El entrenador* = the trainer/coach.

**Example:** *Tengo entrenamiento esta tarde.* — I have training / a workout this afternoon.
