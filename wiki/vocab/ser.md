**Meaning:** to be (identity, characteristics, origin, profession — see [[ser-vs-estar]])

**Pattern:** Highly irregular: *soy, eres, es, somos, sois, son*. Used for identity, origin, profession, characteristics, and time. Preterite: *fui, fuiste, fue, fuimos, fuisteis, fueron* (identical to `ir` — context disambiguates). Imperfect: *era, eras, era, éramos, erais, eran* — see [[past-tense]], [[preterite-irregular-verbs]].

**Example:** *Todos somos honestos.* — We are all honest.
