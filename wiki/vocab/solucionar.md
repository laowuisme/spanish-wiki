**Meaning:** to solve, to fix, to resolve; regular -ar verb

**Pattern:** `solucionar + noun` — e.g., solucionar el problema (solve the problem). Synonym: resolver (more versatile, also means to resolve/decide).

**Example:** *Tenemos que solucionar este problema juntos.* — We need to solve this problem together.
