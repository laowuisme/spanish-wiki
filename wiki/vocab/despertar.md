**Meaning:** to wake (someone) up; *despertarse* = to wake up (oneself); *despierto/a* = awake (adj)

**Pattern:** Stem-changing: *despierto, despertas, despierta*. *despierta* can be the tú imperative ("wake up!") or the adjective "awake" (f).

**Example:** *¿Estás despierta? Tenemos que salir.* — Are you awake? We have to leave.
