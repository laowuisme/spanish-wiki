**Meaning:** cocktail

**Pattern:** Masculine noun (*el cóctel*). Also spelled *coctel* (without accent in some regions). Plural: *los cócteles*. Used for both the drink and a cocktail party (*un cóctel de bienvenida* = a welcome reception).

**Example:** *¿Quieres tomar un cóctel?* — Do you want to have a cocktail?
