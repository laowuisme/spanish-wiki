**Meaning:** fact

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el hecho`, `los hechos`. It appears in `de hecho` ([[de-hecho]], "in fact") and in `es un hecho que...` ("it is a fact that..."). The spelling is the same as the participle of [[hacer]] (`he hecho`, see [[present-perfect]]). An article before it (`el hecho`, `un hecho`) shows that it is the noun.

**Example:** *Eso es un hecho, no una opinión.* — That is a fact, not an opinion.
