**Meaning:** done, made (past participle of `hacer`); also a fact (`el hecho`)

**Pattern:** Two uses. (1) Irregular participle of [[hacer]]: `he hecho`, `has hecho` — see [[present-perfect]]. (2) Masculine noun `el hecho`, "the fact", as in [[de-hecho]]. The note lists the word alone, so the note does not show which meaning you meant.

**Example:** *¿Has hecho la compra?* — Have you done the shopping? / *Eso es un hecho, no una opinión.* — That is a fact, not an opinion.
