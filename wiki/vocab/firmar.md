**Meaning:** to sign (a document, contract)

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. *firmo, firmas, firma*; *la firma* = the signature; *firma aquí* = sign here (tú imperative).

**Example:** *¿Puedes firmar aquí, por favor?* — Can you sign here, please?
