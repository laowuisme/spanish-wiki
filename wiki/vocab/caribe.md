**Meaning:** the Caribbean (the sea and the region)

**Pattern:** Proper noun, so it has a capital letter. It takes the article: `el Caribe`. After `a`, the two words join: `al Caribe`. The sea is `el mar Caribe`. The adjective is `caribeño` / `caribeña`.

**Example:** *Quiero viajar al Caribe en diciembre.* — I want to travel to the Caribbean in December. See [[viajar]].
