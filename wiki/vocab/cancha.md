**Meaning:** sports court or field (Latin American Spanish); Spain equivalent: la pista (tennis/basketball), el campo (football)

**Pattern:** `la cancha de + sport` — e.g., cancha de tenis, cancha de básquet

**Example:** *Vamos a la cancha a jugar.* — We're going to the court to play.
