**Meaning:** or, either

**Pattern:** Coordinating conjunction; becomes `u` before a word starting with *o-* or *ho-* (e.g., *siete u ocho* = seven or eight).

**Example:** *¿Quieres té o café?* — Do you want tea or coffee?
