**Meaning:** pen (ballpoint pen); standard/Spain term

**Pattern:** Noun, masculine: *el bolígrafo*. Often shortened to *boli* in informal speech in Spain. **Regional:** In Mexico and much of Latin America, *[[pluma]]* is used instead. *bolígrafo* is the safe, universally understood formal term.

**Clarification:** *bolígrafo* vs *pluma* — bolígrafo is Spain/standard formal; pluma is Latin America/Mexico.

**Example:** *¿Me prestas tu bolígrafo un momento?* — Can you lend me your pen for a moment?
