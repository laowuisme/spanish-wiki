**Meaning:** to give

**Pattern:** Highly irregular. Key forms: *doy* (yo), *das* (tú), *da* (él/ella), *damos* (nosotros), *dan* (ellos). Preterite: *di, diste, dio, dimos, disteis, dieron* (borrows -er/-ir endings without accents, despite being an -ar verb) — see [[preterite-irregular-verbs]]. Used in many expressions: *dar un paseo* (take a walk), *dar miedo* (to scare), *darse cuenta* (to realise). See [[indirect-object-a-pronoun]] — *dar* often pairs with indirect object pronouns.

**Example:** *Yo te doy los libros.* — I give you the books.
