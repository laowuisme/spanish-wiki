**Meaning:** damaged, hurt (dañado/dañada)

**Pattern:** Past participle of `dañar` (to damage/harm) used as adjective. Classic `estar` + past-participle construction for resultant state — see [[ser-vs-estar]].

**Example:** *El coche está dañado.* — The car is damaged.
