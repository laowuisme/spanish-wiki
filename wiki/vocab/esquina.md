**Meaning:** corner (of a street or room)

**Pattern:** Feminine noun (*la esquina*). Used for street corners and room corners. *En la esquina* = at/on the corner. *Doblar la esquina* = to turn the corner. Plural: *las esquinas*.

**Example:** *El banco está en la esquina.* — The bank is on the corner.
