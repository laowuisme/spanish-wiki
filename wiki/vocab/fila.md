**Meaning:** queue, line; row (of seats/people)

**Pattern:** Feminine noun (*la fila*). *Hacer fila* / *hacer cola* = to queue up. *Cola* is more common in Spain for a queue; *fila* is more common in Latin America and also means a row (cinema, classroom). *En fila india* = in single file.

**Example:** *Hay una fila muy larga en la caja.* — There's a very long queue at the till.
