**Meaning:** before, earlier

**Pattern:** Adverb of time; often paired with `de` (`antes de` + infinitive/noun) or `que` (`antes que` + clause) to introduce what it precedes.

**Example:** *Llegué antes que tú.* — I arrived before you.
