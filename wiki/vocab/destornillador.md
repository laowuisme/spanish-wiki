**Meaning:** screwdriver

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el destornillador`. The word comes from `tornillo` (screw): the tool that "un-screws". It is one kind of [[herramienta]].

**Example:** *El destornillador está en la caja de herramientas.* — The screwdriver is in the toolbox.
