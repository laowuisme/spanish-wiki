**Meaning:** garage (m)

**Pattern:** *el garaje* = the garage; *aparcar en el garaje* = to park in the garage; *garaje comunitario* = shared/communal garage.

**Example:** *El coche está en el garaje — ahora lo saco.* — The car is in the garage — I'll bring it out now.
