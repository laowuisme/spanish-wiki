**Meaning:** armchair

**Pattern:** The `-ón` augmentative suffix marks a bigger, more padded version of `silla` (chair, not yet in the wiki).

**Example:** *Se durmió en el sillón viendo la tele.* — He fell asleep in the armchair watching TV.
