**Meaning:** plaza, bullring, seat

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `la plaza` — a town square; also used for a "seat/spot" (e.g. `plazas disponibles` = available seats) and short for `plaza de toros` (bullring).

**Example:** *Quedamos en la plaza principal.* — We're meeting at the main square.
