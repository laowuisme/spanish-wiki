**Meaning:** mean, wants

**Pattern:** Third-person singular present indicative of `querer` (e→ie stem change); see [[quisiera-vs-quiere]]. The "mean" sense comes only from the collocation `querer decir` — *¿Qué quiere decir esto?* = "What does this mean?"

**Example:** *Ella quiere viajar a España.* — She wants to travel to Spain.
