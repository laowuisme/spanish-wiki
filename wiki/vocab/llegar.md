**Meaning:** to arrive, to get to, to reach; *llego* = I arrive

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb (but spelling change: *llegar → llegué* in preterite). *llegar a* = to get to a place: *¿Cómo llego a la zona vieja?* = How do I get to the old town? Do not confuse with [[llevar]] (to carry/wear) — see [[confusing-llevar-llegar]].

**Example:** *¿Cómo llego a la zona vieja desde aquí?* — How do I get to the old town from here?
