**Meaning:** truth; (as tag question) right?, isn't that so?

**Pattern:** Feminine noun `la verdad` ("the truth"); also used alone at the end of a sentence as a tag question — `¿verdad?`.

**Example:** *Eso no es verdad.* — That's not true. / *Vives aquí, ¿verdad?* — You live here, right?
