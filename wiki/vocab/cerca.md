**Meaning:** near, close (by)

**Pattern:** Distance adverb. Opposite of `lejos`. Often `cerca de` = near to. See also `al lado de` (right next to) for a closer degree of proximity.

**Example:** *La parada está cerca de aquí.* — The stop is near here.
