**Meaning:** to keep, to put away, to save

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. Used for both physical storage (putting something away) and digital saving (like English "save").

**Example:** *Voy a guardar la ropa en el armario.* — I'm going to put the clothes away in the closet.
