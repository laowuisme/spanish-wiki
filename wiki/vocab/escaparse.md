**Meaning:** to escape, to slip away, to get away; *se escapa* = he/she escapes/runs away

**Pattern:** Reflexive verb. *me escapo, te escapas, se escapa*. Also used for things slipping away: *se me escapa la palabra* = the word escapes me. Non-reflexive *escapar* = to escape (less common).

**Example:** *El perrito se escapa del jardín cada vez que abres la puerta.* — The puppy escapes from the garden every time you open the door.
