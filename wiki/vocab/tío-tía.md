**Meaning:** uncle / aunt; also colloquially (Spain): dude / guy / girl

**Pattern:** Family vocabulary. `tío` = uncle / dude; `tía` = aunt / girl (colloquial). In Latin America, colloquial uses differ.

**Example:** *Mi tío vive en Madrid.* — My uncle lives in Madrid. / *¡Tío, qué suerte!* — Dude, how lucky! (Spain, colloquial)
