**Meaning:** to share

**Pattern:** Regular -ir verb. *comparto, compartes, comparte*; *compartir algo con alguien* = to share something with someone.

**Example:** *Compartimos el paraguas porque había una tormenta.* — We shared the umbrella because there was a storm. *Ella comparte su comida con su hermano.* — She shares her food with her brother.
