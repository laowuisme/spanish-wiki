**Meaning:** bakery

**Pattern:** Shops vocabulary. Suffix `-ería` = shop/place where the thing is sold. Related: `pescadería` (fishmonger), `pastelería` (pastry shop).

**Example:** *Compro el pan en la panadería.* — I buy bread at the bakery.
