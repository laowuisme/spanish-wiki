**Meaning:** to climb (a mountain or a rock wall, with effort)

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb: *escalo, escalas, escala, escalamos, escaláis, escalan*. Participle: `escalado` — see [[present-perfect]]. [[subir]] means "to go up" in general. `escalar` suggests a hard climb. It also has a figurative use: `escalar posiciones` (to move up in a ranking).

**Example:** *Mi hermano escala montañas los fines de semana.* — My brother climbs mountains on weekends. See [[montaña]].
