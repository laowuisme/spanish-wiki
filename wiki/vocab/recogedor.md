**Meaning:** dustpan

**Pattern:** Masculine noun: `el recogedor`. From `recoger` (to pick up, to collect).

**Example:** *Usa el recogedor y la escoba para limpiar la cocina.* — Use the dustpan and broom to clean the kitchen.
