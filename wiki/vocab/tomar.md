**Meaning:** to take; to drink; to catch (transport)

**Pattern:** Regular `-ar` verb; means "to take", "to drink", or "to catch" (transport): `tomar el autobús`. In the affirmative tú command, an object pronoun attaches to the end: `tómala` / `tómalo` = "take it" (fem/masc). See [[affirmative-commands]].

**Example:** *¿Quieres tomar algo?* — Do you want to drink something? *Aquí está tu café — tómalo antes de que se enfríe.* — Here's your coffee — take/drink it before it gets cold.
