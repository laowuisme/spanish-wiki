**Meaning:** slowly

**Pattern:** Formed from *lenta* (feminine of [[lento]]) + *-mente* suffix. This is the standard Spanish adverb formation: adjective (fem.) + -mente. Synonym: *despacio* (more colloquial).

**Example:** *Habla lentamente, por favor.* — Please speak slowly.
