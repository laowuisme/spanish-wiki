**Meaning:** I suppose (that); I guess

**Pattern:** First person of `suponer` + `que` + clause. `suponer` has an irregular `yo` form: `supongo`, as `poner` gives `pongo`. The phrase states a guess that you think is probably true.

**Example:** *Supongo que tienes razón.* — I suppose you are right.
