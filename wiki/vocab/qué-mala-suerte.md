**Meaning:** what bad luck

**Pattern:** [[qué-exclamations]] pattern combined with the noun phrase *mala suerte* (bad luck) → *¡Qué mala suerte!*

**Example:** *¡Qué mala suerte, perdiste el tren!* — What bad luck, you missed the train!
