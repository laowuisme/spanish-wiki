**Meaning:** spider

**Pattern:** Feminine noun: `la araña`, `las arañas`. The article stays feminine for a male spider. The `ñ` is a separate letter, not `n`. Other small animals in the same note: [[mosca]] (fly), and [[hormiga]] (ant) from an earlier note.

**Example:** *Hay una araña grande en la pared.* — There is a big spider on the wall.
