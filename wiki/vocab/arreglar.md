**Meaning:** to fix, to repair; also to arrange or tidy up

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb taking a direct object for either sense: `arreglar el coche` (fix the car), `arreglar el cuarto` (tidy the room).

**Example:** *Voy a arreglar el sillón roto este fin de semana.* — I'm going to fix the broken armchair this weekend.
