**Meaning:** ever; at some time

**Pattern:** Common in questions with the present perfect: `¿Has + participle + alguna vez?` Short answers: `nunca` ([[nunca]]), `una vez`, `muchas veces`. `alguna` is the feminine form of `alguno` ([[alguna]]) and agrees with `vez` (feminine). See [[present-perfect]].

**Example:** *¿Has escalado una montaña alguna vez?* — Have you ever climbed a mountain?
