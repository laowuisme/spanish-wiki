**Meaning:** to tell (a story, news); to count

**Pattern:** Stem-changing verb, o → ue in the present: `cuento`, `cuentas`, `cuenta`, `contamos`, `cuentan`. The preterite is regular: `contaron` (they told). In `me contaron que...` Spanish needs no subject word for "they". See [[past-tense]].

**Example:** *Me contaron que eras muy alegre.* — They told me that you were very cheerful. (your sentence in the 2026-11-15 note; see [[alegre]])
