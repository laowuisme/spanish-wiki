**Meaning:** cutlery, silverware, utensils (set)

**Pattern:** Plural noun — refers to the full set (fork, knife, spoon). Related to `cubierto` (covered/covered plate).

**Example:** *¿Puedes poner los cubiertos en la mesa?* — Can you set the cutlery on the table?
