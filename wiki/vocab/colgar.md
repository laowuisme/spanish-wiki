**Meaning:** to hang (up), to hang out (laundry)

**Pattern:** Stem-changing verb: o→ue in present tense singular/3rd plural: `cuelgo, cuelgas, cuelga, colgamos, colgáis, cuelgan`. Common with laundry: `colgar la ropa`. Also used for hanging objects on a wall.

**Example:** *Cuelga la ropa en el tendedero.* — Hang the clothes on the drying rack.
