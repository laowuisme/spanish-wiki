**Meaning:** it depends

**Pattern:** Fixed expression, from the verb `depender` (3rd person singular present) — used alone as a reply meaning "it depends".

**Example:** *—¿Vienes a la fiesta? —Depende.* — "Are you coming to the party?" "It depends."
