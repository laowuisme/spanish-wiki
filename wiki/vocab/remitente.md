**Meaning:** sender (of a letter or package)

**Pattern:** Masculine/feminine noun (*el/la remitente*). Postal vocabulary. The opposite is *destinatario/a* (recipient). On an envelope: *Remitente:* [name] = From: [name].

**Example:** *El remitente no puso su dirección.* — The sender didn't write their address.
