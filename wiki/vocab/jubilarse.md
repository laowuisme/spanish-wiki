**Meaning:** to retire (to stop working, usually because of age)

**Pattern:** Regular reflexive -ar verb: `me jubilo`, `te jubilas`, `se jubila`. In the present perfect the pronoun goes before `ha`: `se ha jubilado` ([[present-perfect]], [[reflexive-verbs]]). The participle `jubilado` also means "retired" or "a retiree".

**Example:** *¿Quién se ha jubilado este año?* — Who has retired this year? (your sentence in the 2026-11-15 note, with question marks added)
