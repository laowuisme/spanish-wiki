**Meaning:** to clear up (weather); to clear (a space); to clear one's head

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb. *se despeja* = it clears up; *el cielo se está despejando* = the sky is clearing up. Often reflexive with weather.

**Example:** *Después de la tormenta, el cielo se despejó.* — After the storm, the sky cleared up.
