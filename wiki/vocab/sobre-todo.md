**Meaning:** above all; especially; mainly; most of all
**Pattern:** adverbial phrase — placed before the element being emphasized
**Example:** *Me gusta viajar, sobre todo a países de habla hispana.* — I like travelling, especially to Spanish-speaking countries.

Also seen standalone as an emphatic tag: *Sobre todo hoy.* — Especially today. (raw/2026-07-22_session.md)
