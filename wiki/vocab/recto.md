**Meaning:** straight

**Pattern:** Adjective, agrees in gender/number (`recto/recta/rectos/rectas`). Fixed direction phrase: `todo recto` = "straight ahead."

**Example:** *Siga todo recto y el banco está a la derecha.* — Go straight ahead and the bank is on the right.
