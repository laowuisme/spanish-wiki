**Meaning:** as soon as possible; the sooner the better

**Pattern:** Adverbial phrase, usually at the end of the sentence. The same meaning as `lo antes posible`. See [[antes]].

**Example:** *Necesitamos firmar el [[contrato]] cuanto antes.* — We need to sign the contract as soon as possible.
