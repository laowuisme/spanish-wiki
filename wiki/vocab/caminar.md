**Meaning:** to walk, walk

**Pattern:** Regular -ar verb (camino, caminas, camina...)

**Example:** *Camino al trabajo todos los días.* — I walk to work every day.

Flagged on 2026-10-24 in the clarification "andar VS caminar". `caminar` is the neutral word for walking as an activity or a route. [[andar]] has more uses: to go around, to be doing something, to work (of a machine).
