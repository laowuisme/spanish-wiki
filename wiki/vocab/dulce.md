**Meaning:** sweet

**Pattern:** Invariable for gender (ends in -e); adds -s for plural (dulce/dulces). Can also be used as a noun meaning "a sweet/candy" (see [[dulces]]).

**Example:** *El café está muy dulce.* — The coffee is very sweet.
