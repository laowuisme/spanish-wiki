**Meaning:** blind date

**Pattern:** Fixed noun phrase built on [[cita]] ("date/appointment") + `a ciegas` ("blindly").

**Example:** *Mi amiga me organizó una cita a ciegas el sábado.* — My friend set me up on a blind date on Saturday.
