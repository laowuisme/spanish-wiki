**Meaning:** until this afternoon; see you this afternoon; goodbye (when you'll meet again in the afternoon)

**Pattern:** *Hasta + time expression* = farewell or "until [time]". Other common forms: *hasta luego* (see you later), *hasta mañana* (see you tomorrow), *hasta pronto* (see you soon).

**Example:** *¡Hasta la tarde!* — See you this afternoon!
