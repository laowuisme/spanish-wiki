**Meaning:** someone, somebody

**Pattern:** Indefinite pronoun. Invariable. Negative counterpart: `nadie` (nobody). Requires personal a when direct object: `busco a alguien`.

**Example:** *¿Hay alguien en casa?* — Is there someone at home?
