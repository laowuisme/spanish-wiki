**Meaning:** room / bedroom (also: hotel room)

**Pattern:** Rooms vocabulary. `Habitación` is the most general word for "room" and is commonly used for bedroom. `Sala` = living room, `habitación` = bedroom/room.

**Example:** *La habitación está al final del pasillo.* — The room is at the end of the hallway.
