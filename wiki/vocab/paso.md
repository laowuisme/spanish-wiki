**Meaning:** step; pace; passage; also: I pass (yo form of *pasar*)

**Pattern:** Masculine noun (*el paso*). Multiple uses: *dar un paso* (to take a step), *paso a paso* (step by step), *el paso del tiempo* (the passage of time). As verb yo-form: *Paso por tu casa.* — I pass by your place.

**Example:** *Da un paso adelante.* — Take a step forward.
