**Meaning:** you hug me (2nd-person singular present of *abrazar*, "to hug," with direct object pronoun *me*)

**Pattern:** Direct object pronoun *me* placed before the conjugated verb; infinitive is *abrazar*.

**Example:** *Cuando estoy triste, me abrazas.* — When I'm sad, you hug me.
