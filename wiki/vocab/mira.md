**Meaning:** look! (tú imperative of mirar); hey, listen (discourse marker)

**Pattern:** *mira* = tú imperative of *mirar* (informal). *mire* = usted imperative (formal). This mirrors the pattern in [[disculpe-vs-disculpen]]: informal commands drop the -s from tú present, formal commands use subjunctive form. Also used as a discourse filler: *mira, lo que pasa es que...* = Look, the thing is...

**Clarification:** *mira* (informal, one person) vs *mire* (formal, one person) vs *miren* (formal, group)

**Example:** *Mira, no es tan difícil.* — Look, it's not that hard. | *Mire usted, necesito hablar con el médico.* — Look here, I need to speak with the doctor.
