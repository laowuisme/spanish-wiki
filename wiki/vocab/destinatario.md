**Meaning:** recipient, addressee (of a letter or package)

**Pattern:** Masculine/feminine noun (*el destinatario* / *la destinataria*). Postal vocabulary. Opposite of *remitente* (sender). On an envelope: *Destinatario:* [name] = To: [name].

**Example:** *El destinatario no estaba en casa.* — The recipient was not at home.
