## What Goes Wrong

Confusing *cuánto* (how much/how many) with *cuando* (when). Both start with *cu-* and are easy to mix up under time pressure, since neither the meaning nor the accent placement is intuitive without practice.

## Why It Happens

- *cuánto/cuánta/cuántos/cuántas* — interrogative/exclamative "how much/how many," accented (¿Cuánto cuesta? ¡Cuánto me alegro!)
- *cuando* — "when," a conjunction/relative adverb; only accented as *cuándo* when it's a direct or indirect question word ("When are you coming?")

The words point at completely different concepts (quantity vs. time), but their shared *cu-* prefix and similar shape cause repeated mixups.

## Correct Usage

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| cuánto | how much | *¿Cuánto cuesta?* — How much does it cost? |
| cuántas/cuántos | how many (agrees with noun) | *¿Cuántas personas vienen?* — How many people are coming? |
| cuando | when (conjunction) | *Te llamo cuando llegue.* — I'll call you when I arrive. |
| cuándo | when (question word) | *¿Cuándo llegas?* — When do you arrive? |
