## Correct Usage

`tener ganas de` + infinitive means "to feel like doing something". `ganas` is always plural. `de` always comes before the verb.

*¿Tienes ganas de salir esta noche?* — Do you feel like going out tonight?

*Tengo ganas de beber algo.* — I feel like drinking something.

Memory hook: learn it as one block, `tengo ganas de` + verb. The block has no singular form and no other joining word.
