# saber + que clause confusion

## What Goes Wrong

Confusing the *saber que* clause construction with other words/verbs (e.g. *conocer*, *conocido*) when asked to complete or translate a *saber que* sentence.

## Why It Happens

*Saber* + *que* clause ("I know that...") requires recalling the irregular yo-form *sé* and the connector *que* under quiz time pressure, which can get displaced by other recently-seen or similar-sounding vocabulary.

## Correct Usage

| Pattern | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| Sé que + subject + verb | *Sé que ella vive aquí.* | I know that she lives here. |
| Sé que + subject + lo/la/los/las + verb | *Sé que tú lo tienes.* | I know you have it. |

- *Sé que tú lo tienes.* — I know that you have it.
