## Correct Usage

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `corto` / `corta` | short in length or time | *El pelo largo VS corto.* — Long VS short hair. |
| `bajo` / `baja` | short in height; low | *Juan es bajo pero muy rápido.* — Juan is short but very fast. |

Memory hook: learn the opposites in pairs. `largo` ↔ `corto` for length and time. `alto` ↔ `bajo` for height. `corta` is the form for a feminine noun, for example `una fila corta`.
