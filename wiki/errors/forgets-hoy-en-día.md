## Correct Usage

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `hoy` | today (this one day) | *Hoy tengo clase.* — Today I have class. |
| `hoy en día` | nowadays, these days (the present era) | *Hoy en día casi todos usan el teléfono para pagar.* — Nowadays almost everyone uses their phone to pay. |

Memory hook: `hoy en día` is "today in the day(s)": the whole present time, not one day.

`actualmente` also means "at the present time". It is a false friend: it does not mean "actually" (`en realidad`).
