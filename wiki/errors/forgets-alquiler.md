## Correct Usage

English uses one word, "rent", for the money and the action. Spanish uses two words.

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `alquiler` | rent; rental (the noun) | *El alquiler en esta ciudad es muy caro.* — Rent in this city is very expensive. |
| `alquilar` | to rent (the verb) | *Decidimos alquilar un apartamento más pequeño.* — We decided to rent a smaller apartment. |

Memory hook: the **alquiler** is the thing you pay each month. **Alquilar** is what you do to get it.
