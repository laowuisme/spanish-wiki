## Correct Usage

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `espantoso` | frightening, dreadful, awful — describes the thing | *Te parece espantoso este color.* — This color seems dreadful to you. |
| `espantoso` (intensifier) | terrible, extreme | *Hace un frío espantoso.* — It is terribly cold. |
| `asustado` | scared — describes the person who feels it | *Estaba muy asustada.* — She was very scared. |

Memory hook: the root is `espanto` (fright). `espantar` means "to scare away". `espantapájaros` is the scarecrow: the thing that scares the birds. `espantoso` is the same family: a thing that gives fright, so it is "frightening" or "awful". The ending `-oso` means "full of", as in [[peligroso]].

Contrast: `espantoso` describes the cause (`el ruido es espantoso`). `asustado` describes the person (`estoy asustado`).
