## Correct Usage

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `compartir` | to share | *Compartimos el paraguas porque había una tormenta.* — We shared the umbrella because there was a storm. |
| `comparar` | to compare | *Voy a comparar los precios.* — I am going to compare the prices. |

The two verbs share their first six letters (`compar-`).

Memory hook: **com + partir**. `partir` means to split, so `compartir` is "to split something together".
