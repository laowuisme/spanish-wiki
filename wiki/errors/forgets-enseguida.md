## Correct Usage

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `enseguida` | right away; in a moment | *Voy enseguida, dame un minuto.* — I'm coming right away, give me a minute. |
| [[ahora]] | now | *Ahora no puedo hablar.* — I cannot talk now. |
| [[luego]] | later; then | *Hablamos luego.* — We will talk later. |

Memory hook: `seguir` (not yet created) means "to follow". `en-seguida` means "in a following": the next thing comes right behind the first one, with no gap. A waiter says `¡Enseguida!` when your order is next.

Spelling: you can also write `en seguida` as two words. Both forms are accepted.
