# perdimos vs pedimos

## What Goes Wrong

Confusing *perdimos* (we lost) with *pedimos* (we asked for / we ordered). The stems *perd-* and *ped-* look similar and both are first-person plural preterite forms.

## Why It Happens

Both verbs follow the same preterite conjugation pattern for -er/-ir verbs, and their stems differ by only one letter (*d* vs *d* in the middle). Under time pressure, the wrong form slips out.

- *perder* (to lose) → *perdimos*
- *pedir* (to ask for, to order) → *pedimos*

## Correct Usage

| Verb | Meaning | Preterite nosotros |
|---|---|---|
| perder | to lose | perdimos |
| pedir | to order / ask for | pedimos |

- *Perdimos el partido.* — We lost the match.
- *Pedimos la cuenta.* — We asked for the bill.
